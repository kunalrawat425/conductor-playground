## The Hard Truth About Zero Audience

Polls, "drop a comment below", "which do you prefer" — all useless with zero followers.
Nobody answers a poll from an account with 12 followers. It looks dead and hurts credibility.

**Cold-start rule:** Every piece of content must earn DISCOVERY before it earns ENGAGEMENT.
Discovery comes from:
1. **Reels** — Instagram shows Reels to non-followers. Carousels do not get distributed.
2. **Facebook Groups** — Post value into groups where your audience ALREADY exists.
3. **Location tags** — Tag Hiranandani Estate, Lodha Splendora etc. Locals browse these.
4. **Hyperlocal hashtags** — People in Thane actually search these.
5. **Outreach comments** — Comment thoughtfully on big Mumbai food accounts.

Polls and engagement CTAs only work after you have 200+ followers. Until then: Reels, Groups, Location tags.
