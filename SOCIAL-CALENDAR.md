Slide 1: **"4 things the packaged fish aisle won't tell you"**

Slide 2: **The pack date vs the catch date**
> That "packed on" date on your supermarket fish? It's when it was packaged — not when it was caught.
> A fish can be 4 days old and packed yesterday.

Slide 3: **The ice glaze trick**
> Fish fillets are sometimes dipped in ice water to add weight and appear fresher.
> A ₹500/kg fish can carry 80–100g of water weight you paid for.

Slide 4: **"Fresh" is not a regulated term**
> In India, there is no legal definition of "fresh fish" in retail.
> A seller can call any fish "fresh" regardless of when it was caught.
