# Relifish Pricing and Monetization Plan

## 1. Pricing Strategy

### Commission Structure: Tiered by GMV

A flat commission punishes small sellers and under-captures value from large ones. Use a tiered model that rewards volume:

| Monthly Seller GMV | Commission Rate |
|---|---|
| Up to Rs 50,000 | 5% |
| Rs 50,001 - Rs 2,00,000 | 7% |
| Rs 2,00,001+ | 8% |

**Rationale:** Start low to onboard local fishing port sellers (traditional fishing communities in local fishing communities who are price-sensitive. As sellers grow dependent on the platform, value capture increases.

**Payment terms:** Settle to sellers within 24 hours. This is a hard competitive advantage over mandis (wholesale markets) that settle in 3-7 days.

### Delivery Fee Model: Distance-Based with Free Threshold

| Order Value | 0-3 km | 3-7 km | 7-12 km |
|---|---|---|---|
| Under Rs 499 | Rs 39 | Rs 59 | Rs 79 |
| Rs 499 - Rs 999 | Rs 19 | Rs 39 | Rs 59 |
| Rs 1,000+ | Free | Free | Rs 29 |

**Free delivery threshold: Rs 1,000.** This is critical -- it pushes AOV upward. Mumbai's average fish purchase at a local market is Rs 400-600. By making free delivery kick in at Rs 1,000, you nudge basket sizes up by 40-60%.

**No delivery beyond 12 km at launch.** Hyperlocal means hyperlocal. Start with 3-4 zones: Bandra-Khar-Santacruz, Andheri-Versova, Dadar-Matunga, and Colaba-Fort. Expand zone by zone.

### Pre-Order Pricing: Discount Model (3-5% Off)

Pre-orders placed by 8 PM for next-morning delivery get a 3-5% discount. This is not generosity -- it is operational efficiency monetized:

- Sellers know demand before they go to sea or to the wholesale market
- Reduces wastage (fish is the most perishable category)
- Enables better route optimization for delivery
- **Formula:** Pre-order discount = (wastage cost saved + route optimization savings) x 0.5

At 5% wastage cost on a Rs 800 order (Rs 40) and Rs 15 route savings, the platform saves Rs 55 and passes Rs 40 back as discount. Net positive.

### Bundle Pricing Mechanics

**"Thali Bundles"** -- curated combinations based on how Mumbai households actually cook:

| Bundle | Contents | A la carte price | Bundle price | Savings |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekend Fry Pack | 500g Bombil + 500g Surmai + Masala | Rs 850 | Rs 749 | 12% |
| Curry Combo | 500g Rawas + 250g Prawns + Kokum | Rs 1,100 | Rs 949 | 14% |
| Party Pack | 1kg Pomfret + 1kg Prawns + 500g Surmai | Rs 2,800 | Rs 2,399 | 14% |

Bundles serve three purposes: increase AOV, move slower-selling species alongside popular ones, and create a differentiated product that cannot be price-compared item-by-item against competitors.

### Dynamic Pricing: Species-Based Surge

During high-demand windows (Friday evenings, festival eves, monsoon scarcity), apply a transparent surge:

- **Tier 1 surge (moderate demand):** +10-15% on premium species (Pomfret, Surmai, Rawas)
- **Tier 2 surge (high demand):** +20-25% on premium species
- **Never surge on budget species** (Bombil, Bangda, small prawns) -- these are the everyday staples of middle-class Mumbai households. Surging on them destroys trust.

Display surge as: "High demand pricing -- Rs 120 above usual price." Transparency prevents backlash.

## 3. Revenue Streams: Now to Future

### Stream Details

**Stream 1 -- Transaction Commission (5-8%):** Core revenue from Day 1. Tiered as described above. No minimum fee per transaction.

**Stream 2 -- Delivery Fees:** Collected from customers, partially offset by rider costs. Net contribution: Rs 5-15 per paid delivery. Free delivery orders are a cost center offset by higher AOV.

**Stream 3 -- Seller Subscription (Month 4+):**
- **Free tier:** Basic listing, standard placement
- **Silver (Rs 999/month):** Priority placement in search, basic analytics (top-selling items, peak hours), early access to pre-orders
- **Gold (Rs 2,499/month):** Featured seller badge, full analytics dashboard, dedicated account manager, promotional slots

**Stream 4 -- B2B/Institutional (Month 6+):** Restaurants, caterers, cloud kitchens, and office canteens. Separate ordering interface. Higher AOV (Rs 3,500+), lower commission (4-5%) but higher volume. Contract-based pricing with weekly invoicing.

**Stream 5 -- Advertising (Month 9+):** Promoted listings in search results and category pages. CPC model: Rs 2-5 per click. Sellers bid for top placement. Start with 1 promoted slot per 10 organic results, scale to 2 per 10.

**Stream 6 -- Data/Insights (Month 18+):** Market pricing data sold to fishing cooperatives, government fisheries departments, and commodity traders. Anonymized demand patterns, species pricing trends, seasonal forecasting. Subscription: Rs 10,000-25,000/month per institutional buyer.

## 4. Competitive Pricing Analysis

### Price Comparison (per kg, cleaned)

| Species | Local Market | FreshToHome | Relifish Target | Relifish Advantage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Surmai (Seer) | Rs 800 | Rs 899 | Rs 780 | -2.5% vs market |
| Pomfret (White) | Rs 1,200 | Rs 1,349 | Rs 1,150 | -4.2% vs market |
| Rawas (Salmon) | Rs 600 | Rs 699 | Rs 580 | -3.3% vs market |
| Bombil (Bombay Duck) | Rs 250 | Rs 299 | Rs 240 | -4.0% vs market |
| Prawns (Medium) | Rs 500 | Rs 549 | Rs 480 | -4.0% vs market |
| Bangda (Mackerel) | Rs 200 | Rs 249 | Rs 190 | -5.0% vs market |

**Why Relifish can be cheaper:** FreshToHome operates a centralized cold chain with warehouses -- their cost structure includes warehousing, fleet, and a 20-25% markup. Local markets have 2-3 middlemen between boat and buyer, each adding 10-15%. Relifish connects local fishing port sellers (who buy directly at auction or catch themselves) to consumers, eliminating 1-2 middlemen. The savings are real, not subsidized.

### Price Sensitivity Analysis

Mumbai fish buyers fall into three sensitivity bands:

1. **Price-first (45% of market):** Housewives, retired seniors, lower-middle-class households. Will switch platforms for Rs 20-30 difference per kg. Must win on Bangda, Bombil, and small prawns.
2. **Convenience-first (35%):** Working professionals, dual-income families. Will pay Rs 50-80 premium for reliable delivery and consistent quality. Win on freshness guarantee and delivery reliability.
3. **Quality-first (20%):** Affluent households, serious home cooks. Will pay Rs 100-150 premium for specific catch details (boat-to-table provenance, catch date, handling method). Win on transparency and storytelling.

**Strategic implication:** Price the bottom 5 species (by volume) aggressively to capture the price-first segment. Margin comes from premium species and bundles sold to the other two segments.
