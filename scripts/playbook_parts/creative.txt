## 1. HERO CAMPAIGN: "KNOW YOUR FISHERMAN"

### Big Idea

Every fish in Mumbai has a story — and a face behind it. Swiggy made you love your delivery partner. Relifish makes you love your fisherman. We put real local fishermen and fisherwomen front and center: their names, their boats, their catch, their craft. In a world of anonymous supply chains, Relifish is radically personal. When you order from Aai Kamla at Sassoon Dock or Raju Bhai from Versova local fishing port, you're not buying fish — you're backing a family.

### 5 Ad Executions

**Execution 1: "Kamla Aai, Sassoon Dock. 32 years at sea."**
Visual: Portrait of a weathered, smiling local independent woman in a nine-yard sari, standing on the docks at golden hour. Fish crates surround her. Her name is typeset large — like a movie poster credit.
Copy: "You know your barista's name. You know your Uber driver's rating. Isn't it time you knew who catches your Surmai? Meet Kamla Aai. She's been fishing before most apps were born. Now she's on Relifish. Order her catch today."
CTA: "Meet your fisherman on relifish.store"

**Execution 2: "Raju Bhai's Pomfret > Your Ex's Promises"**
Visual: Split-screen. Left: a glistening whole Pomfret, beautifully lit like a jewellery ad. Right: Raju, a young local fisherman grinning on his boat, giving a thumbs-up.
Copy: "Finally, something in Mumbai that delivers on its promise. Raju Bhai's white Pomfret. Caught at 4 AM. On your plate by dinner. No drama, no disappointment."
CTA: "Fresh is not a claim. It's a person. relifish.store"

**Execution 3: "The 4 AM Economy"**
Visual: Dark, atmospheric shot of fishing boats heading out into the Arabian Sea under moonlight. A single lantern glows.
Copy: "While Mumbai sleeps, your dinner is being caught. 4 AM. No alarm clock — just the tide. Relifish connects you directly to the people who start their day before yours ends. Pre-order tomorrow's catch tonight."
CTA: "Order tonight. Eat tomorrow's freshest. relifish.store"

**Execution 4: "From Her Boat to Your Plate. Zero Middlemen."**
Visual: A visual timeline strip — boat > dock > Relifish app screenshot > a home kitchen plate. Each panel is a real photo, documentary-style.
Copy: "Old way: Fisherman → Agent → Wholesaler → Retailer → You (and the fish is 3 days old). Relifish way: Fisherman → You. That's it. Fresher fish. Fairer price. Real faces."

**Execution 5: "Not Farm-Raised. Sea-Raised."**
Visual: Underwater shot of the Arabian Sea. A school of Bangda swimming. Overlaid text feels like a nature documentary title card.
Copy: "No ponds. No pellets. No factories. Just the Arabian Sea, a net, and a fisherman named Suresh who's been doing this since he was twelve. Wild-caught Mumbai fish. Only on Relifish."

- Bold color background (ocean blue / sunset orange / Hyperlocal red)

## 3. CAMPAIGN: "PRE-ORDER TOMORROW'S CATCH"

### Communicating the Pre-Order Feature

The core insight: nobody else lets you reserve fish before it's caught. This is Relifish's unfair advantage. We frame it as the most confident thing a fish marketplace can say — "We'll catch it for you."

### Before/After Comparison

| OLD WAY | RELIFISH WAY |
|---|---|
| Wake up at 6 AM | Order from bed at 10 PM |
| Fight the crowd at the market | It comes to you |
| "Bhaiya, aaj Surmai hai kya?" "Nahi" | You already reserved yours |
| Bargain. Argue. Settle. | Fair price. Fixed. Done. |
| Hope the fish is fresh | Know exactly when it was caught |
| No idea who caught it | See the fisherman's profile |
| Carry stinking bags on the train | Clean delivery to your door |

### User Journey Story

**"Priya's Friday Night Surmai"**
9:45 PM Thursday — Priya is scrolling Instagram. A Relifish notification: "Pre-order Friday's catch. Surmai likely available." She taps. Opens relifish.store. Sees Vinod from Versova — 4.8 stars, 200+ orders. She pre-orders 1 kg Surmai, cleaned and cut.

4:00 AM Friday — Vinod heads out. The sea is generous. Surmai is plentiful.

6:30 AM — Vinod docks. Priya's fish is tagged.

9:00 AM — Priya gets a notification: "Your Surmai has been caught. Vinod is preparing your order." With a photo of her actual fish.

12:00 PM — Delivered. Ice-packed. Cleaned exactly how she asked.

7:30 PM — Surmai fry, lemon, thecha on the side. Priya posts it. Tags @relifish.

## 4. CAMPAIGN: "MUMBAI'S FISH MARKET, NOW ON YOUR PHONE"

### Nostalgia + Modernity Angle

Mumbai and fish are inseparable. The smell of Bombil drying in the sun, the chaos of Sassoon Dock, aajis haggling over Pomfret. Relifish doesn't replace this culture — it extends it. We show the old and new side by side, never in opposition. The message: the tradition lives on, just with better tools.

### Hyperlocal Culture Celebration

Partner with Hyperlocal communities for authentic representation. Feature:
- The Hyperlocal language and phrases in ads ("Aila" as a brand expression)
- Traditional Hyperlocal patterns as design elements in the app and packaging
- Hyperlocal festival sponsorship (Narali Pournima — coconut day, the start of fishing season)
- A "Hyperlocal Kitchen" content series — recipes from Hyperlocal homes, cooked by local independent families, using fish from Relifish

### Community-First Messaging

"We didn't build a fish app. We built a bridge between local fishing port and your kitchen."
"Every order supports a fishing family. Not a warehouse."
"Mumbai was built by its fishermen. Order from them directly."

### Photo Series Concept: "Faces of the Catch"

Commission a documentary photographer (think Raghu Rai school). Black-and-white portraits of real Relifish fishermen and fisherwomen.

- Kamla Aai sorting Bombil at dawn, hands blurred with motion
- A young Hyperlocal boy learning to mend nets, his father behind him
- Three generations of a fishing family standing on their boat
- A fisherwoman's hands, close-up, holding a Surmai — weathered, strong, beautiful
- The Sassoon Dock at 5 AM, wide shot, fog and fish and chaos

These run as full-page newspaper ads, Instagram carousels, and as framed prints at Relifish pop-up events. Each photo carries a single line:
"She's been feeding Mumbai for 30 years. Now she's on Relifish."

### Topical/Trend-Jacking Playbook

- **Rain starts in Mumbai?** Instant post: "Monsoon aa gaya. Bangda season bhi. Order before it sells out."
- **IPL match day:** "Mumbai Indians ya Chennai, fish toh Mumbai ka hi best hai. Order your match-day fry."
- **Celebrity fish moment:** Any Bollywood celeb posts a fish dish? Quote-tweet: "Great taste. Would be even better with Relifish Surmai. Just saying."
- **Temperature discourse:** "Mumbai mein 38 degrees? Only thing that should be on fire is your tawa. Bombil fry time."
- **Any "fresh" discourse online:** Hijack the word. "You want fresh takes? We'll do you one better — fresh Pomfret."

### Dos and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Use Bambaiya Hindi / Marathi slang naturally
- Reference real Mumbai locations (Sassoon Dock, Versova, Mahim)
- Self-deprecate (brand humility wins)
- Respond to comments in meme language
- Credit meme formats / collaborate with meme pages

**Don't:**
- Mock fishing communities or their labor
- Use dead fish in gross/shock-value ways
- Make non-veg vs veg jokes (don't alienate, don't preach)
- Force a meme that doesn't fit the brand
- Post stale memes — if the trend is over 48 hours old, skip it

## 6. UGC CAMPAIGN

### #MyRelifishCatch Campaign

**Mechanic:** Every customer who orders is encouraged to post a photo of their fish — raw or cooked — with #MyRelifishCatch. Weekly winner gets free fish for a month. Monthly grand prize: a boat trip with a Relifish fisherman (the ultimate experience).

**Activation:**
- Insert a card in every delivery: "Show us your catch. Tag @relifish. Win free fish."
- App prompt after delivery: "How did it turn out? Share a photo!"
- Re-share best posts on the official page with the customer's name

### Customer Fish Photo Contest

**"Plate vs. Tawa"** — Two categories:
1. Best raw fish photo (the "Plate" — presentation of the fresh catch)
2. Best cooked fish photo (the "Tawa" — the final dish)

Monthly judging by a guest chef. Prizes: Relifish credit, branded merch, a cooking session with the judge.

### Seller Spotlight Series

Weekly feature: one Relifish fisherman, their story, their specialty catch, their family. Told in their voice (Marathi with subtitles). 60-90 second reels. Customers who order from the featured seller that week get a handwritten thank-you note (in Marathi).

### Recipe Challenge

**"One Fish, Five Ways"** — Relifish sends the same fish (e.g., Rawas) to five food creators. Each makes a different dish. Audience votes on the best. Winner's recipe gets added to the Relifish app as an official recommendation on that fish's product page.

## Final Notes for the Creative Team

**Brand voice summary:** Talk like a Mumbaikar who loves fish. Not a corporation that sells it. Be warm. Be funny. Be real. Never talk down to the audience. Never sanitize the messiness of fish — the smell, the scales, the chaos of the market. That's the charm. Relifish doesn't replace the fish market experience. It brings the best part of it (fresh fish from real people) and removes the worst part (the hassle). Every piece of creative should make someone either hungry, proud to be from Mumbai, or both.

**One metric that matters:** Does this creative make someone open relifish.store within 10 seconds? If not, start over.
