## 2. Launch Week (Days 1-7)

### Day-by-Day Plan

**Day 1 (Monday) -- Soft Launch.** Open ordering to first 50 waitlist signups only via WhatsApp broadcast: "You're in. Order now." Monitor every order personally. Call each buyer after pickup for feedback. Instagram post: "Day 1. 50 families. Fresh fish." **Goal: 20-30 orders. Zero complaints.**

**Day 2 (Tuesday) -- Expand to 100.** Open to next 50 waitlist members. WhatsApp broadcast #2. Instagram story: Customer reaction videos from Day 1 buyers. **Goal: 35-45 orders.**

**Day 3 (Wednesday) -- Free Delivery Offer Goes Live.** Announce publicly: "First 100 orders get FREE delivery." Post on Instagram, share in all WhatsApp groups. Open ordering to full waitlist. Instagram Reel: "From the dock to your kitchen in 4 hours." **Goal: 50-60 orders. Hit free delivery cap by end of week.**

**Day 4 (Thursday) -- Press Day.** Send press release + press kit to all 20 media targets. Follow up via DM/email. Founder does 3-4 phone interviews. Instagram: "Why I quit my job to sell fish in Mumbai." **Goal: 40-50 orders + 2-3 press pickups.**

**Day 5 (Friday) -- "Fresh Fish Friday" Inaugural Edition.** First pop-up at Linking Road, Bandra. Table with ice display, QR codes, free tastings of grilled Bangda. Instagram Live walk-through. **Goal: 50-60 orders + 30 new signups.**

**Day 6 (Saturday) -- Instagram Live Fish Auction.** 11 AM Live from a seller's stall. Auction format: show a 2 kg Surmai, "first person to comment SOLD gets it at dock price + free delivery." Do 5 auctions over 30 minutes. **Goal: 500+ live viewers, 60-70 orders.**

**Day 7 (Sunday) -- Community Day.** "Sunday Feast Special" bundle deal. Share customer testimonials. Instagram carousel: "Week 1 by the numbers." Send thank-you WhatsApp with 10% off code. **Goal: 70-80 orders. End week at 300-400 total.**

### Launch Offer Economics

Delivery cost per order: ~50-80. Total cost for 100 orders: 5,000-8,000. Track via WhatsApp status updates: "67/100 free deliveries claimed!" Creates urgency and FOMO.

### WhatsApp Broadcasts

- **Day 1:** "Relifish is LIVE. You're one of the first. Order fresh fish now: [link]."
- **Day 3:** "First 100 orders get FREE delivery. [X] spots left."
- **Day 5:** "Fresh Fish Friday -- we're at Linking Road today. Come taste free grilled Bangda."
- **Day 6:** "TOMORROW 11 AM: Live Fish Auction on Instagram. Dock-price Surmai, first come first served."

90-second vertical Reel. Sequence: Black screen ("Your supermarket fish is 3 days old") -> 4:30 AM boats at Versova dock -> fish being sorted, glistening Surmai -> Hyperlocal fisherwoman: "Aaj Surmai bohot fresh hai" -> phone screen showing Relifish app order flow -> delivery boy on bike -> kitchen, family opens package -> dinner table, fried Surmai, family eating -> Logo + "No middlemen. No markup." + QR code.

Shot on phone. Cost: 0 if founder does it, 3,000-5,000 with a friend and a gimbal. Distribute on Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp status, housing society groups.

## 5. Partnership Strategy

### Restaurant Partnerships (B2B Pilot) -- Week 3+

Target 5-10 small restaurants / seafood thalis / fish fry joints. Pitch: "Same sellers, same fresh fish. Order on Relifish instead of waking up at 5 AM." 5% service fee. Target: local thali joints (Gajalee area), fish fry stalls near stations, Malvani/Mangalorean/Bengali restaurants, cloud kitchens, budget hotel kitchens. **Goal: 5 partners by Week 6, each ordering 10 kg/day = 50 kg/day B2B volume. Cost: 0.**

### Cooking School Partnerships -- Week 4+

Target 3-5 cooking classes. Pitch: "We supply the fish. You recommend Relifish to students." Approach: Foodhall Cooking Studio, The Bohri Kitchen, local Mangalorean/Hyperlocal instructors on Instagram. **Goal: 2-3 partnerships, 10-20 new buyers per class.**

### Fish Market Association Partnerships -- Week 2+

Pitch to Versova, Sassoon, Mahim market associations: "We send customers TO your sellers. We're extending their hours, not replacing them." We get: goodwill, standee permission, seller introductions. **Cost: 0 + time.**

### Housing Society Partnerships -- Week 3+

Target 15-20 societies (500+ flats) in launch neighborhoods. Offer exclusive free delivery for residents' first 2 weeks. Place 1 standee in lobby. Do "fish tasting Sunday" at the compound. **Expected: 10-15 signups per society, 5-8 become regulars. Cost: 3,500 (standees + tasting fish).**

### Corporate Office Lunch Partnerships -- Week 6+

Target office complexes in Andheri-Kurla (MIDC, Mindspace) and BKC. Partner with a tiffin service -- Relifish supplies fish, tiffin partner cooks, offices order via WhatsApp. Test with 1 complex first. **Goal: 20-30 daily orders. Cost: 0 upfront, revenue share.**

### Food Blogger Ambassador Program -- Week 2+

Pick 5 from the 20 influencers seeded pre-launch (best engagement). "Relifish Fresh Squad" -- 2 posts/month, receive free 2 kg fish/week. Unique referral code per blogger. 3-month trial, renew top performers. **Cost: ~8,000/month for 5 ambassadors.**
