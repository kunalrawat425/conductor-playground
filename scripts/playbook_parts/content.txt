## 1. Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Fresh Catch of the Day (30% of content)

**Rationale:** The single most powerful desire-driver in seafood marketing. When someone sees glistening Surmai at 7am, they want it for dinner. This pillar converts attention into WhatsApp DMs and waitlist signups. It also proves daily that Relifish has real sellers with real fish, not a vaporware landing page.

**Formats:**
- Daily Reel (10-15 sec): seller holding today's catch at the dock, text overlay with species + area
- Daily Story (3x): morning catch photo, midday "X sold out" urgency, evening pre-order close reminder
- Twice-weekly carousel: "This week in Mumbai fish" — 5 slides, one species per slide

**Posting frequency:** 6 feed posts/week, 18 stories/week

---

### Pillar 2 — Meet the Seller (20% of content)

**Rationale:** The existential differentiator. FreshToHome is faceless. Swiggy is a logo. We show the actual human who caught your fish. Seller stories build trust faster than any testimonial badge. The koli community is proud, photogenic, and deeply connected to Mumbai's identity — this pillar borrows their authenticity.

**Formats:**
- Weekly Reel (30-45 sec): seller profile — name, neighborhood, years fishing, signature species
- Weekly photo post: seller portrait + bio caption
- Story highlights: "Sellers" folder — pin every seller intro permanently
- Monthly long-form Reel (60-90 sec): "A day in the life" documentary style

**Posting frequency:** 2 feed posts/week, 5 stories/week

---

### Pillar 3 — Fish Education (20% of content)

**Rationale:** Save-worthy content that gets bookmarked, shared, and indexed by Google. This is the long game — builds authority, drives organic discovery, and positions Relifish as the fish knowledge brand in Mumbai. Educational posts have 3x the save rate of promotional posts on Instagram food accounts.

**Formats:**
- Weekly carousel: species guides, freshness tests, name charts
- Biweekly Reel: "How to tell if..." visual tests
- Blog post (SEO): long-form versions of carousel topics
- Infographic stories: quick-reference cards

**Posting frequency:** 2 feed posts/week, 5 stories/week

---

### Pillar 4 — Mumbai Food Culture (15% of content)

**Rationale:** Non-promotional reach. People share content about their city, not about a brand. This pillar taps into Mumbai pride, koli history, monsoon rituals, and street food culture. It earns followers who aren't ready to buy yet but will remember us when they are. Also the best pillar for creator collaborations.

**Formats:**
- Weekly Reel: city food stories, koli history, restaurant spotlights
- Biweekly photo post: Mumbai fish culture moments, dock life, market shots
- UGC reposts: customer meals, tagged photos
- Story polls and quizzes: "Name this fish", "Which area has the best fish fry?"

**Posting frequency:** 1-2 feed posts/week, 5 stories/week

---

### Pillar 5 — Waitlist & Launch (15% of content)

**Rationale:** Every business needs a conversion pillar. This is where we explicitly ask people to join the waitlist, share social proof (X buyers in your area), announce new neighborhoods going live, and create urgency. Without this, the other pillars build awareness that never converts.

**Formats:**
- Weekly Reel: social proof numbers, area launch announcements, countdown
- Weekly carousel: "Why Relifish exists" problem-solution, comparison posts
- Daily story CTA: link sticker to relifish.store
- Launch day hype Reels: pinned per neighborhood

**Posting frequency:** 1-2 feed posts/week, 7 stories/week

## 7. Creative Templates & Visual Identity

### Post Template Descriptions

**Template A — "Catch of the Day":** Full-bleed vertical fish photo on ice. Top 20% translucent dark overlay with white text (area + date). Bottom 30% species list in bold white, price in gold. Bottom strip: logo left, WhatsApp number right. Ocean blue accent.

**Template B — "Meet the Seller":** Seller photo takes 70% of frame at their stall/dock. Bottom 30% white card: name, area, years, signature species. Warm color temperature.

**Template C — "Education Carousel":** Cover slide: white text on ocean blue. Interior: white background, photo top, text bottom, one fact per slide. Final slide: warm orange with white CTA.

**Template D — "Comparison":** Split-screen or table layout. Blue headers, white cells, gold accent for Relifish column. Data-forward, clean.

**Template E — "Story CTA":** Full-bleed photo, dark gradient at bottom. Large text overlay, link sticker bottom-center.

**Template F — "Launch Announcement":** High energy, fast cuts, text animations. Notification ping sound. End card: area name + "ORDER NOW."

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Ocean Blue | #0077B6 | Primary brand. Carousel covers, headers, buttons |
| Deep Navy | #023E8A | Text on light backgrounds, overlays, logo |
| Warm Orange | #F4845F | CTAs, price highlights, urgency elements |
| Seafoam | #90E0EF | Secondary backgrounds, educational content |
| Sand White | #FAF3E0 | Card backgrounds, text areas, newsletter |
| Charcoal | #2B2D42 | Body text, captions |
| Gold | #F0A500 | Price tags, special offers, festival content |

**Rules:** Blue + White for trust. Orange + White for conversion. Blue + Orange never together as backgrounds. Photos always warm-toned (sunrise dock light).

### Photo Style Guide

**Lighting:** Natural light only. 5-7am golden hour at the dock is hero lighting. No flash. Evening warm kitchen light for cooking shots.

**Angles:** Fish on ice at 45-degree overhead (shows sheen + eye clarity). Seller portraits at eye-level, slightly wide (person + context). Cooking shots: overhead for prep, 45-degree for plating, macro for texture.

**Props:** Ice always present in raw fish shots. Banana leaf under fish for market feel. Steel plate for home-cooking (not fancy ceramic). Always show human hands.

**Avoid:** Supermarket plastic packaging shots. Overly styled food-blogger aesthetic. Stock photos. White backgrounds. Filters that make fish look unnaturally vibrant.

**Phone camera settings:** Portrait mode OFF for fish (blurs detail). HDR ON for dock shots. Video: 1080p, vertical. Clean the lens before every shot.
