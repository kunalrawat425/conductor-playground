## WEEKLY OPTIMIZATION (Every Monday)

1. Spend vs pacing
2. CPA per ad set -- kill if >2x target for 5 days with 30+ clicks
3. Top 3 / Bottom 3 creatives -- graduate winners, kill losers
4. Frequency >3.5 on Meta = fatigue, refresh within 48 hours
5. Neighborhood performance -- reallocate toward launch-threshold areas
6. Google search term report -- add negatives weekly
7. WhatsApp conversation-to-waitlist rate

## SCALING RULES

- CPA <70% of target for 7 days: +20% daily budget
- CPA at target, stable: hold
- CPA >150% for 5 days: kill creative, test new
- CPA >200% for 3 days: pause immediately
- Meta frequency >3.5: refresh creative in 48 hours
- Never increase Meta budget >30% in one step (learning phase resets)
- Neighborhood hits 50 signups: trigger launch sequence
- Neighborhood hits 100 orders/month: graduate to maintenance, shift spend to next area

## CRITICAL MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. Do not run ads in neighborhoods without seller pipeline
2. Do not use stock fish imagery -- Mumbai buyers spot it instantly
3. Do not over-target "Koli community" interest -- too narrow, Meta won't spend
4. Do not run English-only ads in Dadar/Prabhadevi/Worli -- Marathi lifts CTR 2-3x
5. Do not skip Google brand defense after press mentions
6. Do not lead with discounts -- attracts deal-hunters, not repeat buyers
7. Do not blast WhatsApp more than 1x/day
8. Do not scale spend before proving unit economics in Neighborhood 1
9. Do not run video without subtitles -- 80%+ watched on mute
10. Do not combine neighborhoods in one ad set -- separate optimization per area

## MEASUREMENT SETUP (Before Spending a Rupee)

1. Meta Pixel verified on relifish.store
2. Custom conversions: waitlist_submit (Lead), first_order (Purchase)
3. Google Ads conversion tag linked to GA4
4. GA4 events: waitlist_submit, first_order, repeat_order, whatsapp_click
5. UTM convention: `utm_source={platform}&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign={name}&utm_content={variant}`
6. WhatsApp tracking: wa.me links with `?text=relifish-{source}-{area}`
7. Offline QR: unique codes per standee location, separate UTMs
