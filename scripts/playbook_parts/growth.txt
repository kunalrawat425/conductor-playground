# Relifish Growth Playbook: Hyperlocal Fish Marketplace, Mumbai

---

## 1. Marketplace Cold Start Strategy

### Solve Supply First

Fish marketplaces are supply-constrained. Mumbai buyers already want fresh fish -- they just hate the experience of going to the mandi. The value proposition is unlocking supply digitally. Without sellers with real inventory, the marketplace is a dead catalog.

**Rule: Get 10 sellers live before spending a single rupee on demand.**

### First 10 Sellers: 14-Day Acquisition Playbook

**Days 1-3: Ground Intelligence**
- Visit Sassoon Dock (5am-7am), Versova local fishing port, and Malad fish market in person
- Identify 30 prospective sellers: look for stalls with smartphones, UPI QR codes, and decent hygiene
- Buy fish from each. Build rapport. Ask: "How much do you sell per day?" and "Do you ever have leftover stock?"
- Photograph their stalls (with permission) for future listings

**Days 4-6: The Pitch (1-on-1, at their stall)**
- Pitch script: *"Aapka fish jo 2pm tak bik nahi paata, usko hum online bech sakte hain. Koi charge nahi pehle 3 mahine. Hum photo lenge, order aayega, aap pack karo, hum pick karenge."*
- Key objection handler: "How will I get paid?" -- Show UPI instant settlement
- Offer: zero commission for 90 days, free listing photography, free packaging materials (branded Relifish bags, cost: ~Rs 5/bag)

**Days 7-10: Onboarding Sprint**
- Goal: 10 sellers with at least 5 SKUs each (50 total listings)
- Send a WhatsApp-trained field agent to each stall to:
  - Take 3 photos per fish variety (whole, cleaned, fillet if available)
  - Set prices (match or undercut local retail by 5%)
  - Set daily availability windows (e.g., "available 6am-12pm")
  - Create their WhatsApp Business connection for order notifications

**Days 11-14: Dry Run**
- Place 20 test orders yourself (friends, family, neighbors)
- Time the full cycle: order placed --> seller notified --> packed --> picked up --> delivered
- Target: under 90 minutes from order to doorstep
- Fix every friction point before opening to real buyers

**Seller Acquisition Channels (ongoing):**
| Channel | Expected conversion | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| In-person mandi visits | 30% | Time only |
| Referral from existing seller (Rs 500 bonus) | 40% | Rs 500/seller |
| WhatsApp groups of fish traders | 10% | Free |
| Local newspaper classified (Navshakti, Loksatta) | 5% | Rs 2,000/ad |

### First 100 Buyers: 21-Day Playbook

**Week 1: Inner Circle (Buyers 1-25)**
- Personal WhatsApp messages to every friend, family member, colleague in Mumbai
- Message template: *"Bhai, maine ek app banaya hai -- Sassoon Dock jaisa fresh fish seedha ghar pe. Pehla order free delivery. Try kar: relifish.store"*
- Post in 10 apartment society WhatsApp groups (Andheri, Bandra, Dadar, Powai -- coastal food communities)
- Target: 25 orders in 7 days

**Week 2: Society Blitz (Buyers 26-60)**
- Partner with 5 housing societies for "Fish Day" -- set up a tasting counter at the society gate on Saturday morning
- Offer: "Order now, delivered in 60 minutes" with Rs 50 off first order
- Distribute flyers with QR code at fish markets (target the buyer, not seller)
- Flyer copy: *"Yeh machli kharidte kharidte thak gaye? Ab phone se mangao. Scan karo."*

**Week 3: Digital Push (Buyers 61-100)**
- Instagram Reels: "Mumbai fish market at 5am" -- raw, authentic content showing the supply chain
- Google Maps listing for Relifish with "Order Online" link
- Hyper-local Facebook/Instagram ads: 2km radius around your delivery zone, targeting 25-45 age, interests in cooking/seafood
- Budget: Rs 5,000 for 1,000 targeted impressions, expected 50 clicks, 10 orders (CAC: Rs 500)

### Liquidity Threshold

The marketplace becomes self-sustaining when:
- **15+ active sellers** with daily inventory updates
- **200+ weekly orders** (enough for each seller to feel meaningful revenue)
- **Repeat rate > 40%** within 30 days (buyers coming back without prompting)
- **Average delivery time < 75 minutes**
- **Zero stockout rate on top 10 SKUs** (Pomfret, Surmai, Bangda, Rawas, Prawns, Bombil, Paplet, Hilsa, Mandeli, Crab)

At this point, word-of-mouth should drive 30%+ of new buyer acquisition organically.

## 2. Viral Loops

### Loop 1: The "Fresh Catch" Share

**Mechanic:** After delivery, show a full-screen card: photo of their fish + seller name + catch location + "Delivered in 47 minutes." One-tap share to WhatsApp/Instagram Story.

**Why it works:** People love showing off fresh food. The delivery speed is the bragging point.

**Copy:** *"Aaj ka catch: Surmai from Versova, delivered in 47 min via @Relifish"*

### Loop 2: Buyer-Invites-Buyer Referral

**Structure:**
- Referrer gets Rs 100 Relifish credit after referee's first order
- Referee gets Rs 75 off first order (minimum order Rs 300)
- Cap: 10 referrals per user per month (prevents abuse)
- Unique referral link via WhatsApp share button

**Copy:** *"Mere wale fishwale try kar. Rs 75 off tera pehla order: [link]"*

### Loop 3: Seller-Invites-Seller

**Structure:**
- Existing seller refers a new seller who completes 10 orders
- Both get Rs 500 bonus credited
- Creates a network effect within fishing communities (Hyperlocal community is tight-knit)

### Loop 4: The Recipe Loop

**Mechanic:** After an order containing Surmai, send a WhatsApp message 2 hours later: *"Surmai Fry Recipe -- 15 min, 5 ingredients"* with a shareable recipe card. Card includes "Order Surmai on Relifish" CTA.

**Why it works:** Recipe content is the #1 shared food content in Indian WhatsApp groups. The recipe is the virus; the fish is the payload.

### Loop 5: The "My Fishwala" Loop

**Mechanic:** After 3 orders from the same seller, badge the seller as "My Fishwala" in the buyer's app. Prompt: *"Share your Fishwala with friends?"* -- generates a personalized recommendation card.

**Why it works:** Personal recommendation of a specific person (not a brand) converts 3x better in Indian markets.

### WhatsApp Virality Tactics (India-Specific)

1. **WhatsApp Catalog Integration:** Every seller gets a WhatsApp Business catalog synced from Relifish. Buyers can browse and order within WhatsApp itself.

2. **WhatsApp Status Updates:** Automated daily status from the Relifish business number: *"Aaj ka fresh stock: Pomfret Rs 650/kg, Surmai Rs 800/kg. Order now: relifish.store"*

3. **Group Ordering:** *"5 logo ka order ek saath? Delivery free!"* -- One person shares a group order link, 4 friends add their items, single delivery.

4. **Morning Market Alerts:** 6:30am WhatsApp broadcast: *"Sassoon Dock se abhi aaya: Rawas (limited), Prawns (jumbo), Bombil. Pehle 20 orders ko free delivery."*

5. **Voice Notes from Fishermen:** 15-second voice note from the actual fisherman: *"Aaj subah 4 baje jaali daali thi, Surmai bahut accha aaya hai."* Authentic, shareable, builds trust.

## 3. Retention Mechanics

### "My Weekly Catch" Subscription

**How it works:**
- Buyer selects preferred fish types, quantity, and delivery day (e.g., every Saturday 10am)
- System auto-orders from their preferred seller (or best available)
- 10% discount on subscription orders vs. one-time
- Easy skip/pause via WhatsApp reply ("Skip this week" / "Haan, bhejo")

**Pricing example:**
- Weekly Catch Basic: 1kg fish (seller's choice) -- Rs 450/week (market: Rs 500+)
- Weekly Catch Family: 2kg mixed fish -- Rs 850/week
- Weekly Catch Premium: 1kg premium fish (Rawas/Surmai) -- Rs 750/week

### Seller Loyalty Program: "Regular Customer" Badges

- After 3 orders from same seller: buyer gets "Regular" badge visible to seller
- After 10 orders: "VIP Customer" -- seller can offer exclusive pricing or first access to premium catch
- Sellers see their regular customer count as a metric: *"Aapke 23 regular customers hain"*

### Push Notification Cadence

| Day | Time | Message | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 9am | *"This week's fresh arrivals are in. Pomfret season is peaking!"* | Awareness |
| Wed | 6:30am | *"Mid-week restock: Prawns and Bombil just listed"* | Mid-week purchase |
| Fri | 7pm | *"Weekend meal planning? Order tonight, delivered Saturday morning"* | Weekend prep |
| Sat | 8am | *"Saturday special: Free delivery on orders above Rs 500"* | Weekend conversion |

**Rules:** Maximum 4 pushes/week. Never push after 8pm. Personalize by past purchase (Surmai buyer gets Surmai alerts). Allow granular opt-out per category.

### Habit Formation: The Saturday Fish Ritual

Target behavior: **Every Saturday morning, open Relifish and order fish for the week.**

Tactics:
1. **Trigger:** Saturday 7am push: *"Aapke Fishwala ne aaj fresh Surmai list kiya hai"*
2. **Routine:** One-tap reorder of last week's order
3. **Reward:** Streak badge -- "4-week streak! Rs 50 off next order"
4. **Investment:** Saved preferences, favorite sellers, address -- switching cost increases each week

### Reactivation Campaigns

**7-day dormant:** WhatsApp message: *"Aapke favorite Fishwala [Seller Name] ne aaj Pomfret list kiya hai. Order karein?"*

**14-day dormant:** Rs 50 off coupon: *"Hum miss kar rahe hain! Rs 50 off on your next order. Code: WAPAS50"*

**30-day dormant:** Personal call from Relifish team: "Kuch problem hua tha kya? Hum improve karna chahte hain." (This is gold for feedback.)

**60-day dormant:** Final attempt: *"Relifish mein bahut kuch naya hai -- 15 new sellers, same-day delivery, and weekly subscriptions. Come back: [link]"*

## 4. Growth Hacking Tactics (Zepto-Style)

### Flash Sales

- **"Pehle 50 orders ko free delivery"** -- Run every Saturday morning. Creates urgency, drives volume.
- **"Bombil Bonanza: Rs 99/500g, only 100 packets"** -- Loss leader to drive app installs. Actual cost Rs 120, subsidize Rs 21 per packet = Rs 2,100 total spend for potentially 100 new users.

### Time-Limited Offers

- **"8am se pehle order karo, 10% off"** -- Trains early morning ordering habit, matches fresh catch availability
- **"Lunch-time flash: 12-1pm, Prawns at Rs 400/kg"** -- Clears afternoon inventory
- **Countdown timer** on the product page for time-limited deals

### Scarcity Signals

- *"Sirf 3kg Pomfret bacha hai [Raju Fishwala] ke paas"* -- Real-time inventory creates urgency
- *"Yeh fish aaj shaam tak nahi milega"* -- Perishability is a natural scarcity driver
- *"12 log abhi yeh dekh rahe hain"* -- Live viewer count on popular listings
- Sold-out badges: *"Aaj ka stock khatam -- kal subah 6 baje naya stock"*

### Social Proof: Live Order Feed

Homepage ticker:
- *"Priya from Andheri ordered 1kg Surmai -- 3 min ago"*
- *"Raj from Bandra ordered 2kg Prawns -- 7 min ago"*
- *"Today: 47 orders delivered in under 60 min"*

Seller pages:
- *"32 orders today from this seller"*
- *"4.8 rating from 156 reviews"*
- *"Raju has been selling on Relifish for 67 days"*

### Gamification

**Order Streaks:**
- 2 weeks in a row: "Machli Lover" badge
- 4 weeks: "Seafood Regular" badge + Rs 50 off
- 8 weeks: "Fish Fanatic" badge + free delivery for a month
- 12 weeks: "Relifish Legend" -- featured on app homepage

**Seasonal Catches:**
- Monsoon ban lift (August): *"First catch of the season! Order Bombil before anyone else"*
- Hilsa season (June-Sept): *"Hilsa Hunter badge -- order Hilsa 3 times this season"*
- Christmas special: *"12 Days of Fish -- a new deal every day"*

**Fish Explorer Badge:**
- Order 10 different fish varieties = "Fish Explorer" badge
- *"You've tried 7 out of 20 fish types. 3 more for your Explorer badge!"*

```
  └── Relifish VIP (top 50 customers, exclusive early access)
```

**Rules:** No spam. Admins post 2-3 times/day max. Members can share recipes, photos, reviews. Every Friday: poll for "Fish of the Week."

### Instagram Strategy

**Content pillars (3 posts/week + daily stories):**

1. **Behind the Scenes (40%):** 5am dock visits, fishermen pulling nets, catch sorting. Raw, unfiltered. No stock photos.
2. **Recipes (30%):** 60-second reels. "Bombil Fry in 10 minutes." Always end with *"Fish from Relifish -- link in bio."*
3. **Seller Spotlights (20%):** *"Meet Raju -- 15 years at Sassoon Dock. His secret: he only sells what he'd feed his own family."*
4. **Customer UGC (10%):** Repost customer photos of their delivered fish/cooked meals.

**Hashtags:** #RelifishMumbai #MumbaiFish #FreshCatch #FishOfTheDay #MumbaiSeafood #HyperlocalFood

### Fish of the Week Program

Every Monday, feature one fish variety:
- Educational post: origin, season, best cooking method, nutrition
- 15% discount on that fish all week
- Recipe video featuring that fish
- Seller who has the best quality of that fish gets a spotlight

**Example:** *"Fish of the Week: Rawas (Indian Salmon). Best: pan-seared with lemon butter. In season now. Rs 680/kg this week (usually Rs 800). Order from Meena Tai -- her Rawas is legendary."*

### Seller Spotlight Series

Monthly video/post for top sellers:
- Their story, how long they've been fishing/selling
- What makes their fish special
- Customer testimonials
- Drives trust and humanizes the marketplace

## 7. Metrics Framework

### North Star Metric

**Weekly orders from repeat buyers.**

This single metric captures supply health (sellers have stock), demand health (buyers are ordering), product quality (they come back), and marketplace liquidity (both sides active). If this number grows week-over-week, everything else follows.

### Input Metrics

| Category | Metric | Target (M6) | Target (M12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supply** | Active sellers (listed in last 7 days) | 70 | 350 |
| | Avg SKUs per seller | 8 | 12 |
| | Daily inventory update rate | 80% | 95% |
| | Seller response time (order acceptance) | < 5 min | < 2 min |
| **Demand** | Weekly active buyers | 500 | 3,000 |
| | Orders per buyer per month | 2.5 | 3.5 |
| | Avg order value (AOV) | Rs 650 | Rs 700 |
| | Cart abandonment rate | < 40% | < 25% |
| **Engagement** | DAU/MAU ratio | 15% | 25% |
| | WhatsApp open rate | 70% | 65% |
| | Push notification CTR | 8% | 6% |
| | Referral invites sent/month | 200 | 2,000 |

### Health Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Healthy Range |
|---|---|---|
| **Liquidity** | % of searches that result in a purchase | > 15% |
| **Seller fill rate** | % of orders fulfilled without substitution | > 90% |
| **Delivery NPS** | Net Promoter Score on delivery experience | > 50 |
| **D7 retention** | % of new buyers who order again within 7 days | > 25% |
| **D30 retention** | % of new buyers who order again within 30 days | > 40% |
| **Seller churn** | % of sellers inactive for 30+ days | < 15% |
| **Time to first order** | Minutes from signup to first order placed | < 10 min |
| **Delivery time** | Order placed to doorstep | < 75 min |
| **Customer support tickets/order** | Ratio of tickets to orders | < 5% |

## Summary: The Three Bets

Everything above rests on three core bets:

1. **Supply wins.** Whoever locks in the best fishermen/sellers in Mumbai's top 5 fish markets wins. This is a ground game, not a tech game. Your moat is relationships with local fishermen.

2. **WhatsApp is the channel.** Not Instagram, not Google Ads. For fish buying in Mumbai, the decision happens in WhatsApp groups and family chats. Every viral loop must end in a WhatsApp share.

3. **Saturday is the moment.** If you own the Saturday morning fish ordering habit for 1,000 Mumbai families, you have a real business. Every feature, notification, and campaign should ladder up to making Saturday morning = Relifish.
