# Appendix: Delivery SOP

## Pickup Model (Default — Month 1-3)

1. Buyer orders on Relifish (app or WhatsApp)
2. Seller receives WhatsApp notification
3. Seller accepts order, packs fish in ice
4. Buyer gets confirmation: "Ready for pickup at [location] by [time]"
5. Buyer picks up, pays cash/UPI to seller
6. Buyer confirms pickup in app → order complete

## Delivery Model (Month 4+)

1. Buyer orders with delivery selected
2. Seller packs in insulated bag with ice pack
3. Delivery rider assigned (nearest available)
4. Rider picks up from seller stall
5. Rider delivers to buyer (max 45 min from pickup)
6. Buyer pays rider (COD) or pre-paid UPI
7. Rider marks delivered → order complete

## Cold Chain Requirements
- Insulated delivery bags: Rs 300-500 each (reusable)
- Ice packs: Rs 20 each (reusable gel packs, freeze overnight)
- Max delivery time: 45 minutes from seller to door
- Max delivery radius: 7km (Month 4-6), expand to 12km later
- Temperature target: fish stays below 4C during transit

## Rider Management
- Start with 2 riders (bike/scooter) per delivery zone
- Pay: Rs 30-50 per delivery (distance-based) + Rs 500/day base
- Rider app: simple WhatsApp-based dispatch initially
- Scale to dedicated rider app at 50+ deliveries/day

# Appendix: Crisis & Negative Review Handling

## Review Response Templates

**1-2 star review:**
"[Name], we're sorry about this experience. This is not the standard we hold ourselves to. We've [action: contacted the seller / issued refund / investigated]. DM us your phone number — we'd like to make this right personally."

**Food safety concern:**
IMMEDIATELY: Contact buyer by phone. Offer full refund. Ask for photo/details. If legitimate concern: suspend seller listing pending investigation. Document everything. Report to FSSAI if required.

**Competitor attack / fake reviews:**
Do NOT engage publicly. Report to Google/platform. Respond calmly: "We don't recognize this order in our system. Please DM us your order ID and we'll investigate."

## Crisis Playbook

**If a buyer gets sick:**
1. Call buyer immediately (within 30 min of complaint)
2. Full refund, no questions
3. Suspend seller listing
4. Investigate: was it the fish? storage? handling?
5. If fish-related: contact all buyers who ordered from that seller that day
6. Document everything
7. Resume seller only after root cause fixed

**If press writes negative article:**
1. Don't panic. Don't respond on social immediately.
2. Draft response with facts (what happened, what you did, what you changed)
3. Respond within 24 hours via official channel
4. Be transparent. Admit fault if at fault.
5. Share corrective action taken

# Appendix: B2B Sales Playbook

## Target Segments

1. Small independent restaurants (Malvani, Mangalorean, Bengali, Goan)
2. Fish fry stalls near stations
3. Cloud kitchens (fish/seafood focused)
4. Hotel kitchens (budget to mid-range)
5. Catering companies
6. Office cafeterias / corporate canteens

## Outreach Process

**Step 1: Identify** — Walk the neighborhood. Note every restaurant with fish on the menu.
**Step 2: Visit** — Go at 3-4 PM (between lunch and dinner, chef is free). Ask for the person who orders fish.
**Step 3: Pitch** — "I supply same-day fresh fish from koli sellers. Dock prices. No middleman. 5 restaurants already ordering. Can I send you today's rate card?"
**Step 4: Trial** — First order at cost price (zero margin). Prove quality + reliability.
**Step 5: Convert** — After 3 successful deliveries, move to regular pricing (4-5% commission).
**Step 6: Lock in** — Weekly standing order. Pre-agreed species + quantities. Invoiced weekly.

## B2B Pricing

| Species | Retail (per kg) | B2B (per kg) | B2B MOQ |
| Surmai steaks | Rs 500 | Rs 420 | 5 kg |
| Pomfret (medium) | Rs 600 | Rs 500 | 5 kg |
| Prawns (medium) | Rs 500 | Rs 420 | 3 kg |
| Rawas fillet | Rs 450 | Rs 380 | 5 kg |
| Bangda (whole) | Rs 180 | Rs 150 | 10 kg |

Commission: 4-5% on B2B (lower than consumer 5-8%, but higher volume).

## B2B KPIs
- 5 restaurant accounts by Month 3
- 10 by Month 6
- Average B2B order: Rs 3,500
- Frequency: 3x/week
- B2B revenue target: 10-18% of total by Month 12
