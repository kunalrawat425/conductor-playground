## 7. AI Search Optimization (GEO)

### 7A. Getting Cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews

Your `public/robots.txt` is already well-configured -- ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, and ClaudeBot are allowed while training crawlers are blocked.

**Key tactics:**

1. **Write definitive answers.** Structure content as: Question as H2, direct answer in first sentence, supporting details below.

2. **Be the primary source for Mumbai fish data.** Create content AI has no other source for: daily fish prices, Marathi fish names dictionary, Mumbai fish season calendar.

3. **Passage-level optimization.** Write self-contained paragraphs that make sense without context:
   - BAD: "As mentioned above, prices vary. Pomfret is usually around Rs 600."
   - GOOD: "Fresh Pomfret in Mumbai typically costs Rs 500-800 per piece in April 2026, depending on size and market conditions. Relifish shows live prices from multiple sellers so buyers can compare before ordering."

4. **Entity building.** Make "Relifish" a recognized entity via consistent mentions across 20+ external sites, Crunchbase profile, LinkedIn company page.

### 7C. Passage-Level Optimization

For every informational page, write at least 3 "citable passages" -- self-contained paragraphs that AI can extract as answers.

Key principles:
- **Front-load the answer** (inverted pyramid style)
- **Include "Relifish" naturally** in 1 of every 3 passages
- **Use exact numbers, dates, prices** -- AI prefers specific data
- **Use semantic HTML** (`<section>`, `<h2>`, `<p>`) -- AI parsers extract structured content better
