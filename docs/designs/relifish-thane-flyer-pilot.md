## Problem Statement

Relifish is a hyperlocal fresh-fish marketplace in Mumbai, with sellers concentrated in Thane. Credible local sellers already have regular customers on WhatsApp. What they want from Relifish is **new buyers**. Relifish has no proof yet that it can find buyers who order once and then come back, and no revenue (0% commission).

The question this plan answers: **can Relifish acquire Thane households, at a known cost, who place a second order through Relifish?**

## Demand Evidence (as of 2026-10-08)

- **Real paid orders: 1.** On 2026-08-30 a buyer paid ₹1,990 via Razorpay to Ocean Lovers Fish. The seller had agreed to list, then rejected the order, and the buyer was refunded. All other orders in the production DB are test data, and the cleanup is in progress.
- **Seller pull is real.** Two named sellers, Fishtokri.com and Ocean Lovers Fish, both say they want new customers from Relifish. Neither will share their existing customer list.
- **Google, last 90 days** (Search Console, `https://www.relifish.store/`):
  - 117 clicks from 1,643 impressions; monthly clicks were 17 in July, 41 in August and 50 in September.
  - 61% of clicks (71) land on `/for-sellers`, from seller-intent queries such as "sell fish online" and "i want to sell my fish".
  - Buyer-intent queries rank near the bottom of page 1 with no clicks: "fresh fish online thane" at position 9.5, "fish delivery thane" at position 8.
  - Searches for seller names land on Relifish seller pages: "fish tokri" / "fish tokri thane" / "fishtokri" add up to 100+ impressions at positions 10–20.
  - Nobody searches for "relifish" yet.
- **GA4, last 90 days** (property 539604989): 123 visits from Google search, about 2 per day. Buyer pages are barely visited (`/shop` 4, `/s/fishtokri-com` 2).

Conclusion: **sellers want Relifish; buyers have not shown up yet.**

## Status Quo

- **Sellers:** take orders on WhatsApp, track them in an Excel sheet, and pack and deliver themselves. They guard their customer list.
- **Buyers:** buy from their usual local seller or market, through a seller's WhatsApp, or from Licious or FreshToHome.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- **Buyer:** households in one Thane cluster, starting with Hiranandani Estate, who buy fish weekly.
- **Sellers:** Fishtokri.com, the **only** seller that delivers to the flyer area (see Coverage below). Ocean Lovers Fish wants buyers but is in Kandivali.
- **Wedge:** one flyer run. 5,000 flyers (₹8,000 for printing and distribution) go out in Thane after Dussehra. The QR code opens `https://relifish.store/shop?utm_source=offline_qr&utm_medium=brochure&utm_campaign=thane&utm_content=delivery`.

## What Relifish does vs. what sellers do (pilot scope)

| Relifish (the platform) | Sellers |
|---|---|
| Discovery: shop page, seller pages, Google presence | Stock and the day's catch |
| Ordering, including catch pre-orders with a min/max price and next-morning reconciliation | Cutting, cleaning, their own packing |
| Payment via Razorpay; automatic refund when the final price is below the max paid | Delivery with their own delivery person |
| Order tracking and buyer notifications (push, email) | Quality of the fish |
| Seller portal: orders, listings, payments | Answering orders through the portal |
| Buyer phone numbers hidden from sellers (branch `buyer-contact-leak`) | |

**Out of scope:** Relifish does not deliver, does not brand packaging (sellers have their own), and does not run WhatsApp support (sellers work through the portal). No discounts, coupon codes, or prices in marketing.

## Constraints

- Flyers are already printed with a single campaign (`thane`), so results cannot be split by society.
- Only one seller (Fishtokri) delivers to the flyer area, with a ₹95 delivery fee and closed on Mondays.
- 0% commission, so Relifish earns nothing during the pilot.
- Sellers will not share their existing customers.
- Brand rules in `SOURCE-OF-TRUTH.md`: no guarantees, no discounts, no prices in content.

## Premises (confirmed with founder)

1. **Revised.** Signing sellers up is easy; getting sellers who actually fulfil orders is hard. The Aug 30 rejection is the evidence.
2. Every Relifish buyer must be acquired by Relifish. Sellers will not share theirs.
3. **Open.** Will acquired buyers move to the seller's WhatsApp? The founder will hide buyer numbers from sellers, but sellers still deliver in person. The pilot measures this (see Success Criteria).
4. Start with one Thane cluster, not all of Mumbai.
5. The flyer run is the first real demand test, and success means repeat buyers, not scans.

## Approaches Considered

- **A. Pilot with what Relifish does today (CHOSEN).** Run the flyers against the current product, measure first orders, repeats and leakage, and decide the business model afterwards.
- **B. Relifish as the shop (deferred to the week-3 decision).** The buyer pays Relifish, Relifish pays the seller minus a 5–10% commission, and the seller sees only an order ID. Deferred because it risks losing sellers before the pilot proves Relifish can bring buyers.
- **C. One morning drop per society (deferred).** Relifish runs one batched delivery per building. Deferred because it conflicts with "Relifish does not deliver" and needs ops staff.
- **Tool for sellers to manage their regulars, Fresho-style (rejected).** Sellers refuse to share customer data; they only want new buyers.
- **Relifish stickers on bags / Relifish WhatsApp support (rejected).** Sellers use their own packing, and support runs through the seller portal.

## Recommended Approach

**Approach A.** It costs nothing beyond the flyers already paid for, uses the current product, and answers the two questions that decide the business model:

1. Can Relifish bring buyers at a known cost?
2. Do those buyers come back through Relifish?

## Success Criteria

**Timeline:**
- **D** = the day the flyers are distributed, after Dussehra (20 Oct 2026).
- **Cohort:** buyers whose *first* paid order is flyer-tagged and placed between D and D+7.
- **Decision date:** D+21, giving every cohort buyer 14 days to reorder.

**Definitions:**
- **Buyer:** identified by the last 10 digits of `buyer_phone`, because `buyer_id` is missing on guest orders.
- **Paid order:** one checkout, i.e. all of a cart's rows placed in the same minute by the same buyer. Paid via Razorpay or a verified payment, and status not `declined` / `cancelled` / `refunded`.
- **Repeat:** a second paid checkout by the same buyer at least 12 hours and at most 14 days after their first, through any channel on Relifish. The 12-hour floor stops a forgotten item added minutes later from counting as a repeat.

| Metric | Works | Inconclusive | Doesn't work |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flyer cohort size (paid first orders) | ≥ 15 | 5–14 | < 5 |
| Cohort buyers who repeat within 14 days | ≥ 5 | 2–4 | 0–1 |
| Flyer checkouts rejected by the seller | 0–1 | 2 | ≥ 3 |
| Cost per paid first order (₹8,000 ÷ cohort) | ≤ ₹550 | ₹551–1,600 | > ₹1,600 |

**Decision rule at D+21.** **Repeats decide** (premise 5).
- Cohort size and cost judge the *channel* (are flyers worth it).
- Seller rejections are a *stop signal*: at ≥3, fix seller reliability before any more spend, whatever the other numbers say.
- If the metrics disagree, follow the repeats row.


- **Works:** run the next cluster and start the pricing conversation (seller pay-per-new-customer, Approach B).
- **Inconclusive:** one more run in the same cluster, distributed door to door through society security instead of the first run's channel, before deciding.
- **Doesn't work:** stop flyers. Test whether the problem is the channel (a society WhatsApp group) or the offer (talk to 10 households in Hiranandani).

**Leakage check (premise 3):** at D+21, call every cohort buyer who did not repeat and ask: *"Did you order from the seller directly?"*
- At least 8 answers are needed before this decides anything; with fewer, leakage stays undetermined.
- If more than half say yes, leakage is the main problem, so favour Approach B or C.
- If most say no, the problem is purchase frequency, so work on re-engagement.

**Pilot query** (run at D+21; replace `D` with the date):
```sql
with paid as (  -- one row per checkout; a cart creates one orders row per line item
  select right(regexp_replace(buyer_phone, '\D', '', 'g'), 10) as buyer,
         date_trunc('minute', created_at) as checkout_at,
         bool_or(utm_campaign = 'thane') as from_flyer
  from orders
  where (razorpay_payment_id is not null or payment_verified_at is not null)
    and status not in ('declined', 'cancelled', 'refunded')
    and not is_test  -- column from migration 071 (branch buyer-contact-leak)
  group by 1, 2
),
firsts as (
  select distinct on (buyer) buyer, checkout_at as first_at, from_flyer
  from paid order by buyer, checkout_at
)
select count(*) as flyer_cohort,
       count(*) filter (where exists (
         select 1 from paid p where p.buyer = f.buyer
           and p.checkout_at >= f.first_at + interval '12 hours' and p.checkout_at <= f.first_at + interval '14 days'
       )) as repeat_within_14d
from firsts f
where f.from_flyer and f.first_at >= date 'D' and f.first_at < date 'D' + interval '8 days';

-- seller rejections of flyer orders: a declined paid order becomes 'refunded', so count both.
-- Also cross-check against the founder's own call log (Dependency 9).
select count(distinct (buyer_phone, date_trunc('minute', created_at))) as rejected_checkouts
from orders
where utm_campaign = 'thane' and status in ('declined', 'refunded')
  and created_at >= date 'D' and created_at < date 'D' + interval '8 days';
```
The query was syntax-checked against production on 2026-10-08 with `utm_campaign` stubbed, because that column only arrives with migration 074.
