Part 1 — What Relifish Is

🎯 What is Relifish?

Relifish is Mumbai's fish marketplace — connecting local fish sellers directly with buyers.

No middlemen. No Swiggy/Zomato markup. Buyers browse real menus from real sellers, compare prices, order fresh catch, pre-order tomorrow's fish, and schedule pickups. Sellers get a free dashboard to manage orders, listings, and operations.

😤 The Problem — Why Fish Buying is Broken

For Buyers

😤 Supermarket fish is 2-3 days old, sitting on ice
✅ Same-day catch from local fishermen
💸 Swiggy/Zomato charges 25-30% markup on fish
✅ Buy direct from seller — fair price, no middlemen
❓ No idea where your fish comes from or who caught it
✅ See seller name, location, ratings, stock

✅ One dashboard — orders, listings, stock in one place

🔔 Missed orders because you were busy cutting fish

Part 4 — Pitch to Onboard

🎣 Seller Onboarding Pitch

Why Join Relifish? (Tell Sellers This)

  • FREE setup — no cost to join, no tech skills needed. We set up your store in 5 minutes.
  • Minimal commission — Swiggy takes 25-30%. We charge almost nothing.
  • Orders even when you're closed — pre-orders come in overnight. You see demand before you go to the dock.
  • Orders even when sold out — buyers pre-order for tomorrow's catch. No lost sales.
  • One dashboard — orders, stock, delivery settings, stats, CSV export. No spreadsheets.
  • Push + email — instant notification on every new order. Never miss one.
  • Online discovery — buyers find you by location. No footfall needed.
  • Schedule your availability — set date ranges + time slots. Buyers book when you're ready.
  • Pause anytime — bulk pause/resume listings. Going to the dock? Pause. Back? Resume.
  • You set the price — pre-orders? You confirm with morning price. Full control.

🛒 Buyer Onboarding Pitch

Why Use Relifish? (Tell Buyers This)

  • Freshest fish in Mumbai — same-day catch from local fishermen. Not 2-3 day old supermarket fish.
  • No markup — direct from seller. No Swiggy/Zomato 25-30% added to the price.
  • See what you're buying — real menus, real prices per kg, seller ratings and location.
  • Compare across sellers — same fish, different prices. Find the best deal without walking to 5 stalls.
  • Shop closed? Sold out? No problem — pre-order for tomorrow. Seller confirms with morning price.
  • Schedule your order — pick a time slot. Seller prepares it when you need it.
  • Track everything — real-time status, push notifications, seller contact. One tap to call.
  • Cancel anytime — pending orders can be cancelled with one tap + optional reason.
  • Save addresses — Zomato-style address book. Pick from saved or add new at checkout.
  • Works like an app — install on phone, works fast, push notifications. No app store.
