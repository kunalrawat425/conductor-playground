# Relifish Social Content Playbook

*Last updated: 2026-04-14*
*Primary platform: Instagram. Secondary: WhatsApp Status + Facebook (cross-post).*

## Strategic Premise

Instagram is where Mumbai's 25–55 food-conscious buyers live — especially via Reels and local food creator follows. We're NOT building a national fish-recipe account. We're building a **hyperlocal, hyperlocal-proud, Mumbai-specific brand** that feels native to the city's food culture.

**North star:** Every post should do one of three things:
1. Make a Mumbaikar say, "I want *that* fish, today."
2. Make someone trust us more than FreshToHome/Swiggy.
3. Drive a waitlist signup or WhatsApp DM (9152207607).

## Content Pillars (5)

| # | Pillar | % Mix | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fresh Catch of the Day | 30% | Desire + freshness proof |

---

### Pillar 1 — Fresh Catch of the Day (30%)

**Goal:** Instant desire. "I want this tonight."

Formats:
- **Reel** (daily, 7am): 10–15 sec. Seller holding today's catch at the dock. Text overlay: "Andheri — today's catch" + species list + price range. CTA: "WhatsApp 9152207607 / link in bio."
- **Story** (3x/day): Morning catch, midday "X species sold out", evening "Tomorrow's pre-orders close at 10pm."
- **Carousel** (2x/week): "This week in Mumbai fish" — 5 slides, one species per slide, size/price/seller.

**Hooks that work:**
- "Caught at 4am. On your plate by 7pm."
- "If you know Pomfret, you'll recognize this one."
- "No supermarket has this today."

---

### Pillar 2 — Meet the Seller (20%)

**Goal:** Build trust and hyperlocal community pride. This is the borrowed-authenticity play — real people, not slick branding.

Formats:
- **Reel** (1x/week): 30–45 sec seller profile. Name, neighborhood, how long fishing, one signature species.
- **Photo post** (1x/week): Seller portrait + bio caption. Tag WhatsApp number.
- **Story highlights**: "Sellers" folder — pin every seller intro permanently.

**Example reel script:**
> *Opens with close-up of hands sorting fish.*
> "I'm Pratik. I fish from Versova. I've done this for 22 years."
> *Cut to catch of the day.*
> "Today: Surmai, Pomfret, Ghol. Direct from me. No middleman."
> *End card:* "Find Pratik on Relifish."

---

### Pillar 3 — Fish Education (20%)

**Goal:** Save-worthy content — buyers actually bookmark these. Drives SEO over time.

Topics:
- "How to tell if fish is fresh" (eyes, gills, smell — 30-sec Reel)
- "Surmai vs Seer fish — same thing? Here's the truth" (carousel)
- "Monsoon fish ban — what's still legal to buy"
- "The 7 best fish in Mumbai, ranked by price-to-freshness"
- "Pomfret sizes explained: baby, medium, export grade"
- "How to clean Bangda at home in 2 minutes" (Reel)
- "Marathi / Hindi / English names for every Mumbai fish" (carousel)

Hook formulas:
- "Stop buying [X] fish. Here's why."
- "Most Mumbai buyers don't know this about Pomfret."
- "Unpopular opinion: frozen fish is fine, except..."

---

### Pillar 4 — Mumbai Food Culture (15%)

**Goal:** Shareability, non-promotional reach, city love. This is what gets us saved + shared by non-buyers.

Topics:
- "5 hyperlocal restaurants in Mumbai worth the trip"
- "The history of Sassoon Dock" (carousel)
- "Bombil fry — the monsoon ritual"
- "How the Hyperlocal community built Mumbai"
- Creator collabs: recipe reels with Mumbai food creators featuring a Relifish seller's catch
- UGC repost: "Tag us when you cook" — customer meal photos

---

### Pillar 5 — Waitlist + Launch (15%)

**Goal:** Conversion — waitlist signups pre-launch, first orders post-launch.

Formats:
- **Reel** (1x/week): Social proof — "127 people in Bandra are already on the waitlist. Join before launch for 10% off."
- **Carousel** (1x/week): "Why we exist" — 6 slides: problem → Relifish → how it works → offers → CTA.
- **Story CTA** (daily): Swipe-up / link sticker to relifish.store
- **Launch post** (per neighborhood): "Andheri is LIVE." Hype reel. Pin.

## Content Atoms — Repurposing

Each seller visit or creator collab produces:
- 1 Reel (Pillar 2)
- 3–5 Stories (behind-the-scenes)
- 1 carousel post (profile + catch list)
- 2 Twitter/X-ready captions (for future expansion)
- 1 WhatsApp Status clip
- Thumbnail photos for Meta ad creative

**Always film vertical. Always subtitle. Always include WhatsApp number on final card.**

## Hashtag Strategy

Mumbai-specific + species-specific, capped at 12 per post.

**Core set (rotate):**
`#Mumbai #MumbaiFood #MumbaiFoodie #BombayFoodie #HyperlocalFood #FreshFish #Seafood #FishLoversIndia #Surmai #Pomfret #Rawas #Bangda #Ghol #AndheriFoodie #BandraFood #VersovaHyperlocal #SassoonDock #RelifishMumbai`

**Neighborhood tags (use 2–3 per post based on which area):**
`#AndheriWest #BandraWest #KharWest #Santacruz #Dadar #Prabhadevi #Worli #Borivali #Juhu`

**Don't use:** Generic global tags (#foodporn, #fishoftheday) — wrong audience, Mumbai relevance dies.

---

## Brand Voice on Social

- Direct, warm, Mumbai-casual
- Mix Hindi/Marathi naturally ("Aaj fresh hai", "Seedha hyperlocal se", "Bhaiyya ne bola")
- Never corporate-speak
- Pride, not apology
- Show the seller's face more than our logo

**Emoji usage:** 🐟 🎣 🌊 — yes. 🔥 💯 — sparingly. Mumbai local flavour > generic food emoji.

## WhatsApp as a Social Channel (underrated)

**WhatsApp Status (Broadcast):** Post the same Catch of the Day Reel to WhatsApp Status daily. Reaches your waitlist directly without Instagram's algorithm.

**Group segmentation:**
- Andheri-Juhu-Versova group
- Bandra-Khar-Santacruz group
- Dadar-Prabhadevi-Worli group
- Sellers-only group (operational)

Broadcast cadence: 1 morning catch + 1 evening pre-order cutoff per day.

---

## Engagement Routine (30 min/day)

1. Respond to every DM within 2 hours (10 min) — most are "is my area live yet?" convert to waitlist.
2. Reply to every comment (5 min) — warm tone, thank, tag seller where possible.
3. Engage on 10 Mumbai food creator posts (10 min) — comment with substance, not emoji.
4. Check 3 competitor accounts (FreshToHome, Licious, local Mumbai fish accounts) for patterns (5 min).
