The Problem

Fish markets run blind.
Buyers walk blind.

🐟

Seller: No Demand Signal

Fishermen haul their catch, set up, and hope for buyers. No way to pre-sell. Unsold fish at 2pm = total loss.

🚶

Buyer: Wasted Trips

Buyers travel to the market with no idea what's available. Favorite species sold out? Leave empty or settle.

📱

Zero Digital Layer

No app, no WhatsApp catalog, no price transparency. The entire transaction is walk-in, cash, and trust.

💸

~30% Daily Waste

Perishable inventory with no visibility. Sellers discount aggressively by noon or throw fish away by evening.

The Solution

Relifish connects fish sellers directly with buyers — before they get to the market

For Sellers

  • Post daily catch with photos, species, and pricing
  • Receive pre-orders before market opens
  • Manage orders via seller dashboard
  • Bundle pricing tiers for bulk buyers
  • Schedule pickup slots

For Buyers

  • Browse nearby sellers, see what's available now
  • Pre-order specific species, guaranteed catch
  • Choose pickup or delivery
  • Track order status in real-time
  • Pay on pickup — zero upfront risk

Why Relifish Wins

What makes us different —
and why no one else can do this

1. Only marketplace model

FreshToHome, Licious, BigBasket — all inventory-led. They buy fish, warehouse it, deliver it. We don't touch the fish. We connect sellers directly with buyers. Asset-light, infinite supply.

2. Pre-orders: no one else does this

Buyer orders tonight, seller reserves tomorrow's catch. This is the killer feature — it gives sellers a demand signal they've never had. Reduces waste by 30%+, guarantees buyers get what they want.

3. Sellers ARE the distribution

Each seller has 50-200 regular customers. Onboard one seller = acquire 50 buyers for free. Zero CAC. The product distributes itself through existing trust networks.

4. WhatsApp-native

90% of India's fish trade happens via word-of-mouth and WhatsApp. We're not asking anyone to change behavior — we're giving them a better version of what they already do.

5. Hyperlocal moat

FreshToHome can't replicate local trust. When a buyer knows their fisherman by name, that relationship doesn't transfer to a warehouse. We digitize the relationship, not replace it.

6. ₹70,000 Cr market, zero tech

India's domestic seafood market is massive and completely undigitized at the local level. We're not competing for market share — we're creating a new category.
