The Problem

Two sides of the same broken market

Sellers lose 25% of catch daily

  • No demand signal. Haul catch, set up stall, hope. Unsold fish at 2pm = total loss.
  • No pre-sell mechanism. Can't take orders in advance. First come, first served only.
  • Limited reach. Sellers serve only walk-in customers within 500m of their stall.
  • <10% cold storage for India's total fish production. No buffer for unsold inventory.

Buyers face friction at every step

  • Wasted trips. Travel to market, favorite species sold out, leave empty-handed.
  • No price visibility. Can't compare sellers. Haggling is the only price discovery.
  • Time-locked. Markets close by 11am. Working professionals can't access fresh catch.
  • Trust gap. Delivery apps charge 25-30% markup. "Fresh" often means 2-3 days old.

The core insight: Fish is India's most traded perishable protein — ₹1.7L Cr/year domestically — yet the local supply chain has zero digital infrastructure. Not underserved. Unserved.

The Solution

Relifish is the marketplace layer between
India's fishermen and their customers

For Sellers

  • Daily catch listings — photo, species, price per kg, availability window
  • Pre-orders — receive orders tonight, reserve tomorrow's catch. Demand signal before they go to sea.
  • Seller dashboard — manage inventory, accept/reject, set pricing tiers, track revenue
  • WhatsApp-native — order notifications via WhatsApp. Zero new behavior required.
  • Expanded reach — serve buyers beyond their 500m walk-in radius

For Buyers

  • Browse nearby sellers — see who's selling what, compare prices across sellers
  • Pre-order tomorrow's catch — order at 10pm, reserved at the dock by 5am
  • Pickup or delivery — scheduled slots, location-aware routing
  • Price transparency — per-kg pricing, no hidden markup, direct seller rates
  • Trust — seller profiles with photos, ratings, order history. Know your fisherman.
Unfair Advantages

Six structural reasons why this works
and why incumbents can't copy it

1. Asset-light marketplace

We don't buy, store, or transport fish. Sellers handle fulfillment. Our gross margin = pure platform take rate. Infinitely scalable supply.

2. Pre-orders (nobody else)

Only platform where buyers order tonight and sellers reserve catch tomorrow. Reduces 25% waste. Guarantees species availability. Patent-worthy mechanic.

3. Zero-CAC distribution

Each seller has 50-200 regulars. Onboard 1 seller = acquire 50 buyers free. Sellers ARE the growth engine. Network effects from Day 1.

4. WhatsApp-native

500M+ Indians on WhatsApp. Fish sellers already use it informally. We formalize the workflow they already have. Zero behavior change = zero friction.

5. Hyperlocal trust moat

When a buyer knows Kamla Tai by name, that trust doesn't transfer to a warehouse. We digitize the relationship, not replace it. Defensible by design.

6. Category creation

$930M+ funded warehouses. $0 funded the 25M fishermen. We're not competing for share in an existing category. We're creating the category.
