Haversine Distance (shop.astro)

dLat = (sellerLat − buyerLat) × π/180
dLng = (sellerLng − buyerLng) × π/180
a = sin²(dLat/2) + cos(buyerLat)
    × cos(sellerLat) × sin²(dLng/2)
dist = 6371 × 2 × atan2(√a, √(1−a))
