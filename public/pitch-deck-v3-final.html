The Problem

Fish sellers still run on calls, WhatsApp,
guesswork, and walk-in demand.

Seller problem

  • 📱WhatsApp, Excel & Cold Call limits. Sellers fail to scale orders because managing regular customers over WhatsApp threads, phone calls, and spreadsheets is highly unscalable.
  • 🐛Buggy, non-scalable home-brewed tech. Sellers trying to patch together their own digital tools hit immediate tech bugs, high complexity, and zero scalability.
  • 💸Price fatigue & poor conversion. Endless phone bargaining and daily pricing guesswork lead to customer drop-off and no real conversions.
  • 😰Anxiety of inventory loss. High stress and daily anxiety of unsold fresh catch rotting after 2:00 PM due to zero predictable demand.

Buyer problem

  • 🐟No species variety. Hard to compare options or find a wide variety of fresh fish from a single stall.
  • ⏳Slow or expensive delivery. Standard delivery apps charge heavy markups and take hours or days to deliver "fresh" inventory.
  • ❄️Freshness & trust gap. Traditional apps sell frozen, days-old packets; local markets require early morning walk-in haggling.
  • 🏷️Direct pricing barrier. Buyers pay marked-up reseller prices instead of getting cheaper rates from trusted local sellers with guaranteed terms.

Relifish answer: Fresh fish delivered under 2 hours by connecting buyers directly with trusted local sellers.

The Solution

Relifish as the Solution

For Sellers

  • 🔗Live catalog sharing. Share your digital store/catalog URL link instantly via WhatsApp and social media to capture regular orders.
  • 📊Inventory management. Easy mobile dashboard to adjust morning stock limits, species availability, and prevent rotted waste.
  • ⏱️Shop timing control. Configurable open/closed hours to control exactly when active buyers can place orders.
  • 📅Pre-order & live support. Dual support for instant same-day delivery and reserving tomorrow's catch the night before.
  • 💸Delivery fee & radius config. Granular settings for custom delivery charges, minimum order value, and geo-delivery radius.
  • 📦Order & UPI management. Track order lifecycle, verify digital payment UPI screenshot proof, and manage COD.
  • ⭐Customer ratings & reviews. Direct ratings feed to build trusted local reputation and reward high-quality service.

For Buyers

  • 📍Find nearby sellers. Auto-discover hyper local verified sellers and local fish stalls within 3km of their location.
  • ⏳2-hour fast delivery. Fresh catch prepped, cleaned, and delivered from stall to doorstep in under 120 minutes.
  • 🗺️Step-by-step journey. Clean flow to discover stalls → browse live catch → pay via UPI → track doorstep delivery.
  • 🏷️Best market prices. Direct seller pricing with zero markup or hidden delivery-app fees.
  • ⭐Trusted ratings feedback. Verified seller profiles, customer ratings, and no early chaotic morning market walks.

Yes. The first internal customer is the seller's operating workflow: catalog, order confirmation, payments, delivery, customer memory, and service quality.
