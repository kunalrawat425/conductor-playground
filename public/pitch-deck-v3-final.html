Product + Problem

Fish sellers still run on calls, WhatsApp,
guesswork, and walk-in demand.

Seller problem

  • No demand signal. Sellers buy or hold fish before knowing what buyers want.
  • Operational chaos. Orders sit across calls, WhatsApp, memory, and walk-ins.
  • No customer memory. Repeat buyers, species demand, and peak times are invisible.
  • Growth is manual. Every new buyer requires more coordination, not better systems.

Buyer problem

  • Fresh means inconvenient. Buyers must walk into the market early.
  • Convenience means compromise. Apps add markup and often sell older fish.
  • No easy comparison. Price, seller trust, variety, and freshness are hard to see.
  • Weekly routine is broken. A high-frequency habit still has low digital trust.

Relifish answer: Fresh fish delivered under 2 hours by connecting buyers directly with trusted local sellers.

What Relifish Does

Seller ops first.
Buyer marketplace second.

For Sellers

  • Digital store. Live catalog with photos, prices, bundles, and availability.
  • Order control. Orders, pre-orders, UPI/COD, delivery radius, payment proof, and alerts.

User Question

Are we changing the internal customer
by solving seller operations first?

Answer

Yes. The first internal customer is the seller's operating workflow: catalog, order confirmation, payments, delivery, customer memory, and service quality.

Why it matters

If sellers cannot operate reliably, buyer demand breaks. Relifish makes sellers operationally ready before adding external buyer volume.

Sequence: Internal seller operations tested enough → external buyer demand comes through ads, seller sharing, and local density campaigns.
