A quick guide for Thane residents buying fish during the monsoon ban 🐟

Maharashtra's 61-day fishing ban (June 1–July 31) means wild catch Surmai, Pomfret, and Rawas aren't coming off boats right now.

What IS good to buy right now:

  • Farmed prawns — excellent quality in monsoon
  • Barramundi (Bhetki) — freshwater farmed, meaty
  • Rohu, Catla, Tilapia — river fish, in season

What to avoid: Any vendor selling "fresh Surmai" or "fresh Pomfret" right now is selling cold-storage stock.

Happy to answer any fish questions for Thane residents 🙏

Freshness Test
3 ways to check if your fish is actually fresh. Takes 10 seconds.
1
Press test — flesh springs back fast = fresh. Stays dented = sitting.
2
Eye check — clear and bright = same-day. Cloudy = been a while.
3
Smell test — sea smell only = fresh. Fishy smell before cooking = already off.
