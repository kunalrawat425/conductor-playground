The Problem

Two sides of the same broken market

Sellers lose 25% of catch daily

  • No demand signal. Haul catch, set up stall, hope. Unsold fish at 2pm = total loss.
  • No pre-sell mechanism. Can't take orders in advance. First come, first served only.
  • Limited reach. Sellers serve only walk-in customers within 500m of their stall.
  • <10% cold storage across India. No buffer for unsold inventory.

₹35,000 Crore worth of fish wasted every year due to post-harvest loss. This isn't inefficiency — it's economic destruction.

Buyers face friction at every step

  • Wasted trips. Travel to market, favorite species sold out, leave empty-handed.
  • No price visibility. Can't compare sellers. Haggling = only price discovery.
  • Time-locked. Markets close by 11am. Working professionals can't access fresh catch.
  • Trust gap. Delivery apps charge 25-30% markup. "Fresh" = 2-3 days old.

71% of India's fish is sold through wet markets with zero digital infrastructure. Not underserved — unserved.

Why Now

Five forces converging to create
a once-in-a-decade window

1. Category proven

$930M+ invested in FreshToHome, Licious, Captain Fresh. Consumers buy fish online. But none built for local sellers.

2. WhatsApp ubiquity

500M+ Indians on WhatsApp. Fishermen already run informal groups with regulars. We formalize what exists.

3. UPI = cash-free fish markets

16B+ txns/month. Digital payments mainstream at every fish market in Mumbai. Payment friction = zero.

4. Waste crisis escalating

₹35,000 Cr wasted/year in post-harvest loss. Pre-orders directly solve this. Government incentives aligning.

5. Demand surge

Per capita consumption 2x by 2048. 8.9kg today → 16kg. Supply chain infrastructure isn't keeping up.

6. Smartphone penetration

800M+ smartphones. Even rural fishermen have Android + Jio data. The infrastructure for a mobile marketplace exists today.

The window: Everyone proved consumers want fish online. No one built for the 25 million sellers. That gap won't stay open — first mover with trust in the Koli community wins.

Risks & Mitigation

Every investor question, answered.
Every risk, mitigated.

"What if sellers don't adopt tech?"

WhatsApp-native — zero new app to learn. Order notifications go to a channel they already check 50x/day. We've designed for the lowest possible friction.

"What if a funded competitor copies you?"

FreshToHome/Licious can't — empowering sellers cannibalizes their warehouse model. Quick commerce doesn't care about fish. Our moat = trust with fishing communities, not code.

"What if buyers just stick to their local stall?"

We don't replace the stall. We give the stall a digital storefront. Buyers still buy from the same seller — now they can also pre-order and compare.

"What about food safety & quality?"

Same sellers, same fish, same market — we're not introducing new supply. Ratings + reviews = self-policing quality. Bad sellers lose customers fast.

"Can you scale beyond Mumbai?"

Every coastal Indian city has the identical structure. Mumbai is the playbook; Goa, Mangalore, Kochi, Kolkata are copy-paste with local community trust-building.

"Solo founder risk?"

Built entire marketplace, infra, marketing, and go-to-market pre-funding. Velocity > team size at this stage. Co-founder hire is Use of Funds priority once funded.

"What if take rates are too low to sustain?"

6 revenue streams, not 1. Commission is the anchor; delivery fees, subscriptions, B2B, promoted listings, and market data layer on top. Revenue mix diversifies by M6.

"Why ₹25L? That's tiny."

Intentionally lean. ₹25L = 6 months to break-even + buffer. We've been ₹0-burn until now. We don't need more money — we need 6 months of runway to prove unit economics.
