The Story
Every morning, Kamla Tai hauls 80kg of fresh Surmai to her stall in Sassoon Dock. By 2pm, 20kg is unsold and rotting. She has no way to take orders in advance. No way to reach buyers beyond 500 meters. No cold storage.

This isn't a tech problem. It's a coordination problem. And it's worth $22 billion.

Competitor Autopsy

They raised $930M. Here's why
they still can't serve this market.

FreshToHome — $320M raised

Model: Buy fish from aggregators → warehouse → cold chain → deliver. Replaces fisherman entirely.

Result: ₹421Cr FY25 revenue but still unprofitable. Heavy capital locked in warehouses + cold chain.

Why they can't copy us: Their entire model depends on cutting out the local seller. Empowering sellers = cannibalizing their own supply chain.

Structural conflict — can never pivot to marketplace

Licious — $490M raised, $2B IPO target

Model: Meat-first inventory brand. Fish = 15% of revenue, afterthought category.

Result: ₹845Cr FY25, but losses of ₹500Cr+. Burning cash on chicken/mutton logistics.

Why they can't copy us: Won't rebuild their entire model for a side category. Fish is SKU #47 for them, it's our entire company.

Fish is their side hustle — it's our whole mission

Blinkit / Zepto — $1.4B+ raised

Model: Quick commerce. Fish = frozen packets in a dark store. 25-30% markup over market price.

Result: Fish is 2-3 days old, limited species, no seller relationship. Mumbai buyer pays ₹750/kg for Surmai that's ₹550 at the market.

Why they can't copy us: Dark store model requires cold chain + inventory. Can't onboard 25M individual fishermen.

Convenience play — wrong model for fresh fish

Captain Fresh — $120M+, $1B IPO filed

Model: B2B export to US/EU. 60%+ revenue from international markets. Zero consumer presence.

Result: Doesn't serve Indian consumers at all. Export supply chain is completely different business.

Why they can't copy us: Export logistics ≠ hyperlocal marketplace. Different customer, different supply chain, different economics.

Export business — not even in our market

How It Works

A day in the life of Relifish

Buyer's story

10:00 PM — Priya opens Relifish. Sees 8 sellers near Bandra with tomorrow's catch listed. Surmai at ₹650/kg from 3 sellers. She picks Kamla Tai (4.8 stars, 200+ orders).

10:02 PM — Places pre-order: 1kg Surmai, 500g Bangda. Selects 7am pickup slot.

5:30 AM — Kamla Tai confirms with morning price. Priya accepts with one tap.

7:00 AM — Picks up reserved, cleaned fish. No haggling. No sold-out disappointment.

Seller's story

9:00 PM — Kamla Tai checks WhatsApp. 12 pre-orders for tomorrow. She knows exactly how much Surmai to reserve at the dock.

4:00 AM — At Sassoon Dock. Reserves 15kg Surmai (12 orders + buffer). Confidence, not guesswork.

5:30 AM — Confirms orders with morning prices via dashboard. Buyers notified automatically.

2:00 PM — Zero waste today. All pre-ordered fish was picked up. Walk-ins got the rest. Revenue up 40%.

The magic: Pre-orders close the demand-signal loop. For same-day buyers, 2-hour delivery connects them directly to nearby sellers. Sellers stop guessing, buyers get fresh fish fast, and wastage is eliminated. Everyone wins.

Risks We Own

We don't hide risks. We plan for them.
Here's every one and our mitigation.

RISK: Sellers won't adopt tech

WhatsApp-native. Zero new app. Notifications go where they already look 50x/day. We designed for the lowest-literacy, lowest-friction possible.

PLAN: WhatsApp-only MVP. Graduate to app after trust built.

RISK: Funded competitor copies us

FreshToHome can't — empowering sellers cannibalizes their $320M warehouse model. Blinkit/Zepto don't care about fresh fish. Our moat is trust with the Hyperlocal community, not code.

PLAN: Move fast. Lock in seller relationships before anyone notices.

RISK: Buyers stick to stall visits

We don't replace the stall. We give the stall a digital storefront. Same seller, same fish, same trust. Plus pre-orders and compare.

PLAN: "Upgrade the relationship" messaging, not "replace your guy."

RISK: Solo founder

Built entire marketplace, marketing materials, and GTM playbook pre-funding. Velocity > team size at this stage.

PLAN: Ops hire Month 1. Co-founder search from Day 1 post-funding.

RISK: Low take rates unsustainable

6 revenue streams, not 1. Commission anchors. Delivery, subscriptions, B2B, promoted listings, market data layer on top.

PLAN: Revenue mix diversifies by M6. No single-stream dependency.

RISK: Fish quality/safety issues

Same sellers, same fish, same market. We don't introduce new supply. Ratings + reviews = self-policing quality.

PLAN: Review system live at launch. Bad sellers lose visibility fast.

RISK: Can't scale beyond Mumbai

Every coastal Indian city has identical structure: fish markets, fishing communities, zero digital layer.

PLAN: Mumbai is the playbook. Goa, Mangalore, Kochi = copy-paste + local trust.

RISK: ₹25L is too small to matter

Intentionally lean. We've been ₹0-burn until now. ₹25L = 6 months to break-even + buffer. We don't need more money — we need runway.

PLAN: If burn faster, pivot to cash-only ops. Worst case: break-even by M10.
