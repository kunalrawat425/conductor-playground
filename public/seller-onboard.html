Common questions
What is Relifish?
Relifish is Mumbai's fish marketplace. It puts your fish shop online so buyers across your area can find you, see your prices, and order directly. Think of it as your own website + WhatsApp shop, but easier.
Is it really free?
Yes. First 10 sellers get zero commission forever. After that, we take a small 5-8% commission only when you make a sale. No upfront fees, no monthly charges.
Do I need a smartphone?
Just WhatsApp. All orders come to your WhatsApp. You accept with one message. No app to download, no tech to learn.
How will buyers find me?
When buyers search for fish in your area on Relifish, your shop appears with your fish, prices, and ratings. We also send daily "catch alerts" to buyers near you.
What if I don't have fish some days?
No problem. You update your listing daily — only show what you have. If the sea is rough or you take a day off, your shop shows "closed today."
How do I get paid?
Buyers pay on pickup (cash or UPI). Money goes directly to you. Relifish doesn't hold your payments.
