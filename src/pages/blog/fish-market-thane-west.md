# Fish Market in Thane West: When to Go, What's Fresh, and Today's Rates

**Thane West has local fish markets and neighbourhood fish sellers. Go early in the morning for the widest choice, check freshness yourself, and compare rates, because fish prices change every day.** If you'd rather not go, you can order from a Thane West seller online and have it delivered.

---

## When to go

- **Early morning** has the most choice. The popular fish (surmai, pomfret, prawns) sells first.
- **Weekends and festival days** are busier, and prices are often higher.
- **The monsoon fishing ban** limits sea fish. It runs from June 1; in 2026 it was extended to August 15. Expect less choice and higher prices then. See our [monsoon fishing ban guide](/blog/monsoon-fish-guide-thane-fresh-fish-june-july).

## What you'll usually find

- **Sea fish:** [surmai](/fish/surmai), [pomfret](/fish/pomfret), [rawas](/fish/rawas), [bangda](/fish/bangda), [bombil](/fish/bombil), [halwa](/fish/halwa).
- **Shellfish:** [prawns](/fish/prawns) and [crab](/fish/crab).
- **Freshwater fish:** [rohu](/fish/rohu) and [katla](/fish/katla), which are available even in the monsoon.

## How to check freshness at the stall

1. Eyes are clear, not cloudy.
2. Gills are bright red.
3. Flesh is firm and springs back.
4. It smells of the sea, not sour.
5. Prawns are firm, with heads attached and no black patches.

## How to compare prices

- **Compare per kg.** A "plate" or "piece" price isn't comparable between stalls.
- **Size matters.** Large pomfret or surmai costs more per kg than small.
- **Cleaning and cutting** may be included or charged extra. Ask first.
- **Check today's online rates first.** Our [fish price today](/fish-price-today) page shows live per-kg prices from sellers on Relifish.
