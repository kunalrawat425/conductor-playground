## The Hassle of Traditional Prep: Why Urban Kitchens Compromise

Seafood is highly delicate, and traditional handling methods often create significant friction in modern apartments:

*   **The Prep Mess:** Descaling, gutting, and cleaning fish at home is messy. Scales scatter across your clean kitchen counters, and the waste creates immediate disposal issues.
*   **The Lingering Odour:** Standard packaging leaks. When fish is delivered in simple plastic bags, the moisture leaks, transferring a strong odour to your refrigerator shelves and kitchen garbage.
*   **Uncertain Hygiene:** When you buy from local stalls or standard delivery apps, you have no visibility into how or where the fish was cleaned. Was it prepared with purified water? Were sanitised tools used? 

These worries often lead families to buy seafood less frequently, or settle for stale, pre-packaged supermarket brands that lack texture and natural juices.
