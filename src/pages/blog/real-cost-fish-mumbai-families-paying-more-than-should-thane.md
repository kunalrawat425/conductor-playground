## The Hidden Costs: Beyond the Price Tag

When you opt for convenience through grocery apps or large supermarkets, you're often getting fish that has travelled a long, convoluted path. It's bought in bulk, transported to massive warehouses, stored in cold storage for days, sometimes weeks, and then finally delivered to your door. Each step in this journey adds to the cost – not just monetary, but in terms of freshness, quality, and even potential health risks.

### The Supermarket & App Markup: Your Hard-Earned Money Vanishing

Let's talk numbers. Those convenient apps charge a significant commission, usually 15-30% on seafood, which is then passed directly onto you. Supermarkets have their own overheads – rent, refrigeration, staff – all factored into the price. This means you're paying a premium for fish that's already lost its peak freshness, often sitting on ice for days or, worse, deep-frozen and thawed multiple times. Imagine paying 30-40% more for a product that's already inferior. That's money that could be saved, or better yet, spent on genuinely fresh, flavourful ingredients for your family.

### The Real Cost of "Convenience": Health, Taste & Time Lost

Beyond the financial burden, there's the invisible cost. Stale fish isn't just less tasty; it can be less nutritious. The delicate omega-3 fatty acids, crucial for brain and heart health, degrade over time. The vibrant texture becomes mushy, and the distinct aroma turns unappetizing. Furthermore, many commercial fish suppliers use various treatments to extend shelf life or enhance appearance, raising concerns about chemicals and additives. Are you truly saving time if you're compromising your family's health and sacrificing the joy of a truly delicious meal?

And what about the constant worry? "Will this fish smell up my kitchen?" "Is it really fresh, or just made to look fresh?" "Is it chemically treated?" These anxieties detract from the cooking experience and the enjoyment of the meal. You're losing peace of mind, all in the name of a convenience that often delivers disappointment.
