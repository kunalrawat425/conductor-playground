## The Tides of Change: Why Fish Prices Fluctuate Daily

The daily price of fish, especially sought-after varieties like *pomfret* and *surmai* in Mumbai and Thane, is influenced by several factors that are constantly in motion:

### 1. Supply and Demand: The Ocean's Bounty

Just like any commodity, fish prices are a classic example of supply and demand. If a particular species has a bumper catch on a given day, prices tend to drop. Conversely, if fishing boats return with a sparse haul, prices will inevitably climb. Factors influencing the catch include:

*   **Weather Conditions:** Rough seas, heavy rains, or strong winds can prevent boats from venturing out or force them to return early, significantly reducing the day's catch.
*   **Fishing Seasonality:** Certain fish are seasonal. During their peak season, they are more abundant and generally cheaper. Off-season, they become scarcer and more expensive.
*   **Natural Cycles:** Even moon phases and tidal patterns can influence fish behaviour and catch rates.

### 2. Fuel Prices and Operational Costs

Fishing isn't cheap. The cost of diesel for fishing trawlers, ice for preservation, labour wages, and maintenance all contribute to the final price. When fuel prices spike, so too do the operational costs for fishermen, which are then passed down the supply chain.

### 3. The Middleman Maze: A Traditional Problem

The traditional fish supply chain in India is notoriously long and inefficient. From the fisherman, the catch typically goes through multiple layers: local aggregators, wholesalers at the main *mandis* (like Sassoon Dock or Crawford Market), then sub-wholesalers, and finally to local vendors or retailers. Each middleman adds their margin, sometimes as much as 30-50%, before the fish even reaches your plate.

This multi-layered system also increases the time fish spends out of the water, often necessitating cold storage or chemical treatments to maintain a semblance of freshness. The result? You end up paying more for fish that’s older and potentially less healthy.
