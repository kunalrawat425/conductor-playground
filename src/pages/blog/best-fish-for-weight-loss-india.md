# Best Fish for Weight Loss in India

**The leanest fish commonly sold in Mumbai and Thane are [prawns](/fish/prawns) (65 kcal per 100 g), [bhetki](/fish/bhetki) (68 kcal), [bombil](/fish/bombil) (69 kcal) and [rawas](/fish/rawas) (73 kcal), and [bangda](/fish/bangda) has the most protein of the fish around 100 kcal (21.51 g).** No fish makes you lose weight by itself. What fish does offer is a lot of protein for few calories, as long as it isn't cooked in a lot of oil. ICMR-NIN's guidelines suggest grilling, baking, steaming or sautéing with minimal oil instead of frying.

Every number below comes from a source listed under [Sources](#sources). Values are **per 100 g raw edible portion** (the flesh you eat, without head, bones or shell).

*This is general food information, not medical or dietary advice. If you have diabetes, a heart or kidney condition, or are pregnant, check with your doctor before starting a weight-loss diet.*

**How to read this table**

- All rows except rawas are from IFCT 2017 (ICMR-NIN). IFCT gives energy in kilojoules; we converted to kcal with IFCT's own factor (1 kcal = 4.18 kJ) and rounded.
- Rawas is not in IFCT 2017. Its row is from the Philippine Food Composition Table for the same species, *Eleutheronema tetradactylum*, per 100 g edible portion.
- These are raw values. Cooking changes them: fish loses water, and any oil you add adds calories.

## Lean fish or oily fish: which is better for weight loss?

Both fit. Lean fish (prawns, bhetki, bombil, rawas, rohu) have the fewest calories. Oilier fish (tarli, surmai, pomfret, salmon) have more calories, but most of the extra is fat that includes omega-3: per 100 g, surmai has 1.05 g EPA + DHA, tarli 0.61 g and pomfret 0.59 g, against 0.12 g for prawns (IFCT 2017). ICMR-NIN recommends marine fish for these omega-3 fats.

A practical mix is to eat mostly lean fish and include an oily fish once or twice a week. For more on omega-3, see [omega-3 fish for heart health](/blog/omega-3-fish-india-heart-health).

## How much fish should you eat?

ICMR-NIN's *Dietary Guidelines for Indians* (2024) say:

- Non-vegetarians can get high-quality protein from 700 g to 900 g a week of fish, poultry or lean meat, or from eggs.
- During a weight-loss diet with a daily deficit of 500 to 750 kcal, getting about 15% of energy from protein may help preserve muscle mass.
- Don't cut more than 40% of your daily energy needs, so that muscle is maintained.

How much of that weekly total is fish is up to you. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about the calorie target that is right for you.

## Is fish tawa fry good for weight loss? Tawa fry vs curry vs steamed

ICMR-NIN's guideline on preventing obesity says to choose lean meats such as skinless poultry and fish, and to cook by grilling, baking, steaming or sautéing with minimal oil instead of frying, because this lowers the energy density of the meal. Applied to everyday Indian fish dishes:

- **Steamed or grilled** (paplet in banana leaf, grilled bhetki or rawas): little or no added oil.
- **Curry** (bangda or rohu curry, prawn curry): a good option if you go easy on oil and coconut.
- **Tawa fry with a little oil**: closer to sautéing than to deep frying. A thick rava or batter coating and extra oil add calories.
- **Deep fried**: absorbs the most oil.

On air fryers, ICMR-NIN says air frying uses much less oil than deep frying, but air-frying fish may lower its omega-3 fats and increase some inflammatory compounds; adding herbs may reduce that.

## A simple week of fish meals

There is no fixed plan in the guidelines, so treat this as an example, not a prescription:

- **Monday:** Bangda curry, light on oil, with rice or bhakri and a vegetable
- **Wednesday:** Steamed or grilled pomfret with salad and chapati
- **Friday:** Prawn and vegetable stir-fry with a little oil
- **Sunday:** Rawas or bhetki tawa fry with minimal oil, and dal

## FAQ

**Which fish is best for weight loss in India?**
Prawns, bhetki, bombil and rawas are lowest in calories per 100 g (65 to 73 kcal). Rawas has the most protein for its calories in the table (17.9 g in 73 kcal), and bangda has 21.51 g protein in 101 kcal.

**Is fish tawa fry good for health?**
A tawa fry with a little oil is lighter than a deep fry. ICMR-NIN suggests grilling, baking, steaming or sautéing with minimal oil instead of frying.

**What are the healthiest types of fish to eat?**
For calories, lean fish like prawns, bhetki and rawas. For omega-3, oilier sea fish like surmai, tarli and pomfret. A mix of both is a sensible approach.

**Can I eat fish every day while losing weight?**
ICMR-NIN suggests 700 g to 900 g a week of fish, poultry or lean meat in total for non-vegetarians. If you are pregnant, follow the mercury advice in our [low-mercury fish guide](/blog/low-mercury-fish-india-pregnancy).

**Is fish better than chicken for weight loss?**
Both are lean protein; see our [fish vs chicken comparison](/blog/fish-vs-chicken-protein-per-100g).
