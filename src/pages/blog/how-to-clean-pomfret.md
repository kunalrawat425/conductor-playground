# How to Clean Pomfret at Home

**To clean a pomfret: scale it lightly, cut off the fins, remove the gills, slit the belly and take out the gut, then rinse well and pat dry.** It takes about five minutes per fish with a sharp knife or kitchen scissors. If you'd rather not, most sellers clean and cut pomfret for you when you order.

---

## Before you start: is it fresh?

- **Eyes** are clear and bulging, not cloudy or sunken.
- **Gills** are bright red or pink.
- **Skin** is shiny and silvery (white pomfret) or glossy dark grey ([halwa / black pomfret](/fish/halwa)).
- **Body** feels firm and springs back when pressed.
- **Smell** is clean and of the sea, never sour.

---

## What you need

- A sharp knife or strong kitchen scissors
- A cutting board you can wash easily
- A bowl of cold water
- Salt, or a little lemon or vinegar (optional, to rinse)

---

## Step by step

1. **Rinse the fish** under cold running water.
2. **Scale it.** Pomfret has small scales. Scrape gently from tail to head with the back of a knife until the skin feels smooth.
3. **Trim the fins and tail.** Cut off the top and bottom fins with scissors. Trim the tail into a neat "V" if you're frying it whole.
4. **Remove the gills.** Lift the gill cover and cut out the red gills.
5. **Gut the fish.** Make a small slit just behind the gills along the belly. Pull out the insides and scrape away any dark lining.
6. **Wash well.** Rinse inside and out. A quick rinse in water with a pinch of salt or a little lemon helps.
7. **Score or cut.**
   - **For fry:** make two or three shallow diagonal cuts on each side so the masala gets in.
   - **For stuffed pomfret:** slit along the side to make a pocket.
   - **For curry:** cut across into three or four pieces.
8. **Pat dry** before applying masala. A dry fish fries crisp.

## Quick answers

**Should I remove the pomfret's head?**
It's optional. Keep it for a whole fry. Remove it for curry pieces.

**Does pomfret have many bones?**
No. It has a central spine and few small bones, which is why it's a favourite for children.

**How long does cleaned pomfret keep?**
Cook it the same day for the best taste. Keep it in the coldest part of the fridge until then.
