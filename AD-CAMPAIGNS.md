## Campaign Strategy

Relifish has ₹12,000/month — roughly ₹400/day. This is a precision budget, not a spray-and-pray budget.

**One platform. One format. One CTA.**
Meta Click-to-WhatsApp (CTWA) ads only. Zero friction — tap ad, open WhatsApp conversation, we capture them in our community. No website checkout friction.

**Two phases:**
- Phase 1 (June 15–July 31): Waitlist building — "Join now, be first August 1"
- Phase 2 (August 1+): Order conversion — "Fresh catch is back. Pre-order now."

## What NOT to Do (Confirmed Constraints)

- ❌ No prices in any ad creative
- ❌ No discount codes or coupons
- ❌ No "guaranteed delivery" or "guaranteed fresh" language
- ❌ No claim about competitor prices by name
- ❌ No promotional "50% off" or launch offer framing

**What we DO instead:**
- Lead with trust (honesty about the ban, transparency about sourcing)
- Lead with community (your building, your neighborhood)
- Lead with outcome ("Sunday lunch deserves the real thing")
- Let the WhatsApp conversation do the selling
